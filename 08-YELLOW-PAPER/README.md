## Immutable Principles

The following principles define the foundation of HumChain Network:

- **Genesis = 0 HCN**

  No currency exists before human participation activates the network.

- **No Human, No Money**

  Economic existence begins with verified human participation.

- **One Territory, One Equal Allocation**

  Every activated territory receives an identical protocol-defined allocation, ensuring territorial equality within the global infrastructure.

- **Human Participation Before Economic Expansion**

  The network grows through verified inclusion, not through pre-created supply.
