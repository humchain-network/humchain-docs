# KYC/AML Policy – HumChain Network

## Preamble

HumChain Network is a global economic infrastructure based on an essential principle: a decentralized economy must be built by real human beings.

Proof-of-Humanity is therefore a foundation of the protocol. It ensures that each participant represents a real and unique person, in order to preserve network fairness and prevent manipulation.

However, human trust cannot rely solely on verification of physical existence. A global economic infrastructure must also comply with legal obligations related to fraud prevention, anti-money laundering, and prevention of illicit activities.

This policy defines HumChain Network's general approach regarding:
- Know Your Customer (KYC);
- Anti-Money Laundering and prevention of illicit activities (AML);
- protection of the balance between regulatory compliance and individual sovereignty.

---

### Article 1 – Objective of the KYC/AML Policy

The KYC/AML policy of HumChain Network pursues several objectives:
- protect the economic integrity of the network;
- ensure participants are real persons;
- limit fraudulent behavior;
- allow economic actors to use HumChain in a trusted environment;
- comply with applicable regulatory obligations according to territories.

HumChain does not consider compliance as an obstacle to economic freedom, but as a necessary condition for a sustainable global economy.

---

### Article 2 – Fundamental Principle: Proof-of-Humanity and Compliance

HumChain distinguishes two complementary notions:

**Proof-of-Humanity**
It answers the question: "Does this identity correspond to a real and unique human?" It protects the fundamental principle that each verified human has the same status in the network.

**KYC**
It answers the question: "Can this person legally participate in certain regulated economic activities?"

KYC does not replace Proof-of-Humanity and Proof-of-Humanity does not automatically replace all KYC obligations. Both mechanisms have different but complementary objectives.

---

### Article 3 – Compliance Principles

- **Proportionality**: The verification requested must correspond to the risk level and activity concerned.
- **Minimization**: Only necessary information must be collected.
- **Data Protection**: Information used under KYC must comply with HumChain's Privacy Policy.
- **Territorial Neutrality**: Each territory applies its own regulatory requirements without undermining fundamental equality between verified humans.

---

### Article 4 – Scope of KYC

KYC requirements may vary depending on activities carried out on HumChain Network. They may concern:
- certain regulated financial operations;
- professional commercial activities;
- economic partners subject to legal obligations;
- certain operations related to public or institutional markets.

Simple human participation in the network must not automatically lead to excessive collection of information.

---

### Article 5 – Role of KYC Providers

HumChain Network may collaborate with specialized providers to carry out certain regulatory verifications. These providers must comply with applicable laws, security standards, data protection principles, and requirements defined by HumChain. The KYC provider acts as a specialized compliance actor and not as owner of users' identity.

---

### Article 6 – Territorial Approach

As HumChain is organized around economic territories, each territorial activation must take into account local laws, national regulatory requirements, and obligations applicable to financial or digital services. Territorial representatives have a role of information and support, but do not replace competent authorities or specialized bodies.

---

### Article 7 – Founding Commitment

HumChain Network affirms an essential principle: Compliance must protect the human economy, not create a surveillance economy. The objective of KYC/AML is not to control individuals, but to create an environment where humans, merchants, artisans, producers, and businesses can exchange in a reliable framework. Technology must strengthen trust between humans, while respecting their dignity and sovereignty.

---

### Article 8 – Fraud Prevention

HumChain Network implements a prevention policy aimed at preserving the integrity of its economic infrastructure. This policy pursues in particular the following objectives:
- prevent creation of multiple accounts;
- limit identity theft;
- protect users against fraud;
- preserve fairness of HCN distribution mechanism;
- strengthen trust between participants.

The precise detection and prevention mechanisms will fall under the technical implementation of the protocol and must respect principles of proportionality, data protection, and respect for fundamental rights.

---

### Article 9 – Anti-Money Laundering and Countering Illicit Activities

HumChain Network rejects any use of its infrastructure for money laundering, financing of illicit activities, fraud, or any other activity contrary to applicable laws. When regulation requires it, the protocol or services built on it may implement necessary compliance measures, in collaboration with specialized providers and competent authorities. Application of these measures must always seek balance between regulatory compliance, privacy protection, and respect for user rights.

---

### Article 10 – Responsibilities of Actors

Compliance is based on shared responsibility. Users undertake to provide accurate information when law or protocol requires. Territorial representatives inform and support their community on applicable obligations, without substituting for public authorities or KYC providers. Merchants, producers, artisans, businesses and other economic actors remain responsible for compliance with legal obligations applicable in their respective jurisdictions. KYC providers and specialized partners assume responsibilities incumbent upon them in accordance with concluded contracts and regulations in force.

---

### Article 11 – Cooperation with Competent Authorities

HumChain Network respects decisions of legally competent authorities when rendered in accordance with applicable laws. Any request for data access or implementation of particular measures is examined with respect for fundamental rights of users, protection of personal data, procedural guarantees provided by relevant legislation. HumChain seeks to reconcile legal requirements with its mission to protect privacy and user sovereignty.

---

### Article 12 – Revision of the KYC/AML Policy

Regulations relating to digital assets, digital identity and anti-money laundering are evolving rapidly. This policy may be updated to take into account legislative developments, new international standards, recommendations of competent authorities, evolution of HumChain Network protocol. Any significant modification will be published in official project documentation.

---

### Article 13 – Founding Declaration

HumChain Network considers that regulatory compliance and economic freedom are not incompatible. A sustainable global economy requires both: a unique and verified human identity, privacy protection, respect for laws, transparency of rules, responsibility of each actor. The protocol affirms that trust results neither from generalized surveillance nor from total absence of control, but from balance between individual freedom, collective responsibility, and security of economic infrastructure.

---

### Limitation of this Document

This policy expresses HumChain Network's founding principles regarding KYC and anti-money laundering. It does not constitute an operational compliance manual or technical specification. Detailed procedures, verification tools, risk detection mechanisms, reporting obligations, and implementation modalities will fall under technical teams, compliance officers, specialized providers, and legal requirements applicable in each territory. The role of the founder is to define guiding principles to which these mechanisms must comply.

### Conclusion

The KYC/AML policy complements Proof-of-Humanity principles by ensuring HumChain Network remains an open, responsible, and compliant economic infrastructure. It is part of founding vision: building a global economy where each verified human being has same status, where local economies are strengthened before opening to world, and where technology remains in service of humans rather than opposite.
