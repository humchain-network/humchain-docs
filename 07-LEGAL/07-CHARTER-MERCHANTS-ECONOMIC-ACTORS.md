# Charter of Merchants and Economic Actors – HumChain Network

## Preamble

HumChain Network is a global economic infrastructure whose vocation is to connect local economies in order to build a more open, more inclusive, and more balanced global economy.

Unlike networks primarily designed for exchange of digital assets, HumChain places real economic activity at heart of its development. Wealth is born from work, production, trade, innovation, and cooperation between human beings.

Merchants, artisans, producers, farmers, entrepreneurs, businesses, cooperatives, and institutions constitute main creators of this value. Their participation is essential to realization of HumChain Network mission.

This Charter defines principles and commitments applicable to economic actors using HumChain ecosystem.

---

### Article 1 – Purpose of the Charter
Establishes principles applicable to natural and legal persons who offer, produce, sell, distribute, or purchase goods and services within HumChain Network ecosystem. Complements Constitution, White Paper, Yellow Paper, Terms of Use, and other official protocol documents.

### Article 2 – Mission of Economic Actors
Contribute to development of real economy by using HumChain Network as exchange infrastructure. Participate in: creating value through activity, fostering local exchanges, developing national markets, strengthening regional exchanges, progressively opening activities to international trade. HumChain supports this progression according to founding principle: A strong global economy rests on strong local economies.

### Article 3 – Fundamental Principles
Exercise activities respecting: honesty, transparency, responsibility, quality, respect of contractual commitments, fair competition, respect of applicable laws. Recognize trust constitutes first wealth of sustainable economy.

### Article 4 – Value Creation
HumChain Network affirms economic value is created by human activity. Role of HCN is to facilitate circulation of this value, not replace it. Economic actors commit to promote activities creating wealth, employment, innovation, and sustainable development for their territories.

### Article 5 – Use of HCN
Merchants and other economic actors may use HCN as medium of exchange in accordance with protocol rules and applicable laws. Use of HCN must foster: commercial exchanges, payments, productive investments, economic cooperation. HCN is designed as tool serving real economy.

### Article 6 – Consumer Protection
Economic actors remain responsible for goods and services they offer. Commit to: provide accurate information, respect commitments to customers, handle complaints diligently, respect regulations relating to consumer protection. HumChain Network provides exchange infrastructure without becoming party to contracts concluded between users.

### Article 7 – Development of Local Economies
Encouraged to favor, when relevant, local economic circuits to strengthen value creation in territories. HumChain then favors progressive opening of exchanges to national, regional, then international markets. Approach aims to build more balanced global economy where each territory can develop own wealth before sharing with rest of world.

### Article 8 – Economic Cooperation
HumChain encourages partnerships between citizens, artisans, merchants, producers, businesses, cooperatives, associations, and institutions. Cooperation is considered driver of economic growth, innovation, and resilience of territories. Protocol favors exchanges based on trust, mutual respect, and common interest.

### Article 9 – Social and Environmental Responsibility
Invited to develop activities respecting people, communities, and environment. HumChain encourages initiatives contributing to: job creation, skills development, valorization of local productions, preservation of natural resources, sustainable economic growth.

### Article 10 – Commitment Declaration
By joining HumChain Network ecosystem, each economic actor commits to contribute to development of economy based on trust, responsibility, and value creation. Recognizes true wealth comes from work of human beings, creativity, cooperation, and capacity to produce useful goods and services. Through activity, participates in building global economy where technology facilitates exchanges, but human remains first creator of value.

---

### Limitation of this Document
Defines principles applicable to economic actors of HumChain Network. Constitutes neither commercial contract, nor tax regulation, nor legal framework applicable to professional activities in each country. Specific obligations of merchants, businesses, and institutions remain governed by laws of respective jurisdictions. Role of founder is to define values and principles that will guide entire economic ecosystem of HumChain Network.
