# Charter of Validators – HumChain Network

## Preamble

Validators constitute one of the technical pillars of HumChain Network. Their mission is to protect the integrity, availability, and security of the infrastructure on which the human economy built by the protocol rests.

HumChain Network establishes a fundamental distinction between human governance and technical securing of the network.

Validators do not govern HumChain economy. They guarantee proper functioning of infrastructure so that citizens, merchants, artisans, producers, businesses, developers, and territorial representatives can interact in a reliable, transparent, and decentralized environment.

This Charter defines principles, responsibilities, and commitments applicable to validators.

---

### Article 1 – Purpose of the Charter
This Charter sets principles governing mission of validators within HumChain Network. It complements Constitution, White Paper, Yellow Paper, Terms of Use, and other official protocol documents.

### Article 2 – Mission of Validators
Participate in security and resilience of HumChain Network blockchain:
- secure network;
- validate operations in accordance with protocol rules;
- preserve integrity of blockchain;
- strengthen availability of infrastructure;
- participate in stability of network.

Exercised in respect of rules established by protocol.

### Article 3 – Fundamental Principles
Validators exercise responsibilities according to principles: neutrality, integrity, transparency, responsibility, availability, security. They act in interest of network as a whole and not for benefit of particular group.

### Article 4 – Absence of Governance Power
In accordance with founding principles of HumChain Network:
- validators do not lead protocol;
- they do not represent territories;
- they have no political privilege due to function;
- they do not determine economic policy of network.

Mission consists exclusively of securing technical infrastructure. Human governance of HumChain remains independent from validation function.

### Article 5 – Neutrality
Validator processes all operations in accordance with protocol rules. Prohibited: discrimination between users, voluntary manipulation of network functioning, use of function for personal purposes contrary to general interest. Trust in HumChain rests on impartial infrastructure serving all.

### Article 6 – Responsibilities
Each validator commits to: respect protocol rules; contribute to network security; maintain behavior compliant with function requirements; cooperate with other validators when necessary for proper functioning of infrastructure; report anomalies likely to compromise network security. Technical modalities fall under technical documentation.

### Article 7 – Integrity and Confidentiality
In exercise of functions, validators respect confidentiality of information to which they may legitimately have access. Commit not to exploit position to obtain undue advantage or harm interests of users or protocol. Contribute to maintaining climate of trust essential to development of HumChain Network.

### Article 8 – Responsibility Towards Community
Validators participate in protection of infrastructure used by millions of people called to exchange, produce, undertake, and create value. Recognize mission goes beyond technical dimension: contributes to preserving trust in economy based on Proof-of-Humanity. Therefore exercise functions with rigor, impartiality, and sense of responsibility.

### Article 9 – Suspension or Removal of Validator Status
When validator seriously fails obligations, voluntarily compromises network security, or acts in contradiction with fundamental principles of HumChain Network, he may lose validator status according to rules provided by protocol. Any applicable procedure must respect principles of transparency, fairness, and responsibility.

### Article 10 – Commitment Declaration
By becoming validator, each participant commits to protect HumChain Network infrastructure for benefit of all users. Recognizes mission is not to exercise power over network, but to guarantee it remains secure, resilient, and accessible. Validator is guardian of infrastructure, while verified human beings remain creators of economy.

---

### Limitation of this Document
This Charter defines institutional principles applicable to validators. It does not describe technical validation mechanisms, consensus, hardware requirements, cryptographic parameters, or operational procedures, which fall under technical documentation and development teams. Role of founder is to define values, responsibilities, and principles validators must respect in exercise of mission.
