# Data and Registration Policy - HumChain Network

## Preamble

HumChain Network is a global economic infrastructure based on Proof-of-Humanity. Its objective is to enable every verified human being to participate in a decentralized economy while guaranteeing the uniqueness of each identity and respect for privacy.

HumChain Network considers trust as the foundation of its global economic infrastructure.

Proof-of-Humanity is a central element of the protocol: it ensures that a real human being has only one identity on the network, while preventing manipulations that could compromise the fairness of the system.

Unlike some projects that claim to retain no data, HumChain adopts a transparent approach: certain information must be retained to protect the network against multiple accounts, identity theft, and fraud.

This Policy explains what data is collected, why it is necessary, how it is protected, and what the rights of users are.

The protection of personal data is a fundamental principle of the protocol. No personal data is written to the blockchain.

---

### Fundamental Principles

HumChain applies the following principles:

- no personal data is recorded on the blockchain, even in encrypted form; only a cryptographic proof of validity may be recorded;
- only information strictly necessary for a person's uniqueness, network security, or legal obligations is collected;
- personal data is stored off-chain in systems distinct from the blockchain infrastructure;
- identification data is separated from technical data to limit risks in case of incident;
- each data processing pursues a clearly defined and documented purpose;
- each user is informed before registration of the nature, purpose, and retention of collected data.

---

### Article 1 – Data Protection Principles

1. **Data Minimization**: Only data necessary for protocol operation, network security, and legal obligations is collected.
2. **Separation blockchain / personal data**: No personal data is written directly to the HumChain blockchain.
3. **Privacy Protection**: Personal data is kept off-chain in secure environments with strict access controls.
4. **Transparency**: Clear information before any collection.

---

### Article 2 – Data Collected During Registration

**Country**
Allows linking each person to their activation territory. Necessary for HumChain's territorial model. Retention: yes.

**City**
Allows local organization of the network and facilitates work of territorial representatives. May be deleted or made optional if no longer essential.

**Last Name and First Name**
Collected only when necessary for a regulatory identification procedure (KYC). When no longer useful to the protocol, not retained.

**Verified Phone**
Allows verification of a real user, account security, limitation of multiple registrations. Not stored in plain text when possible. Cryptographic hash preferred.

**Territorial Code**
Confirms user linkage to activation territory. Linked to territory and protocol, not to personal identity.

**Facial Biometrics**
Main mechanism for uniqueness. Rules: no raw photo retained, only irreversible biometric template, liveness check, regular audits for bias. Used exclusively to guarantee human uniqueness, not for commercial or surveillance purposes.

**PIN Code**
6-digit code protecting local wallet access. HumChain never stores this code. Unlocks a cryptographic key stored locally on user device.

**Recovery Code**
Mechanism to allow user to recover wallet access without HumChain taking control of assets. Non-custodial recovery preferred.

**Identity Document (KYC)**
When regulation requires identity verification, performed by specialized KYC provider. HumChain does not retain identity documents. KYC provider applies legal retention obligations specific to jurisdictions.

**Identity Verification (KYC)**
Exact scope defined by protocol governance per regulatory requirements: public sales of HCN, regulated operations, situations imposed by local law. Before activation of a territory, verification of applicable legal requirements. When KYC provider already performs reliable biometric comparison between face and ID document, protocol avoids redundant biometric processing.

---

### Article 3 – Purposes of Processing

Personal data may be used only for:
- creating a unique HumChain identity;
- preventing multiple accounts;
- securing access to the network;
- enabling participation in HCN mining;
- ensuring regulatory compliance;
- protecting users against fraud;
- improving protocol security.

HumChain Network does not sell personal data.

---

### Article 4 – Data and Blockchain

Fundamental principle: The blockchain must prove the validity of an action, not reveal the private identity of a human.

Thus no biometric data, no identity document, no sensitive personal information is written directly to the blockchain. Human identity remains protected off-chain.

---

### Article 5 – Data Retention Period

Principle of limited retention. Each category retained only for period necessary for its purpose. Precise durations defined with legal experts per territorial regulatory obligations.

- Data needed for protection against multiple identities may be retained as long as necessary to preserve human uniqueness;
- Data used for legal obligations retained per imposed durations;
- Data that becomes useless must be deleted or rendered unusable per applicable rules.

Balance between individual right to erasure and collective need to preserve integrity of Proof-of-Humanity.

---

### Article 6 – User Rights

Each user benefits in particular from:
- clear information before any collection;
- explicit consent when required, especially for biometric processing;
- right of access, rectification, erasure within limits compatible with protocol security;
- right to explanation on data use;
- right to withdraw consent;
- right to lodge a complaint with competent authorities.

HumChain will transparently document any limitations between right to erasure and need to prevent multiple accounts.

---

### Article 7 – Special Protection of Biometric Data

HumChain recognizes biometric data as requiring the highest level of protection. It is not commercial data or surveillance tool. It solely answers: How to guarantee that an economic participation corresponds to a real and unique human being?

- Gives no particular economic advantage;
- Creates no public identity;
- Does not allow tracking of personal activities;
- Confers no governance power.

Its sole function is to protect fairness among participants.

---

### Article 8 – Role of External Providers

Collaboration possible with specialized providers for: regulatory identity verification (KYC), data security, compliance audits, biometric system analysis.

These partners must respect HumChain requirements and applicable regulations. They do not become owners of user data.

---

### Article 9 – Security and Responsibility

Measures implemented:
- encryption of data at rest and in transit;
- separation between databases and cryptographic keys;
- strict and logged access control, limited access;
- regular security audits;
- monitoring of unusual activities;
- incident response plan including notification procedures per applicable regulations;
- mechanisms to limit potential biometric biases;
- alternative solutions for persons unable to perform certain liveness tests.

---

### Article 10 – Security Incident and Data Breach

In case of incident:
- analysis of incident;
- limitation of its impact;
- information of affected persons when required;
- cooperation with competent authorities when law requires;
- implementation of corrective measures.

Trust relies on ability to acknowledge, manage, and correct incidents transparently.

---

### Article 11 – Data Governance

Data management respects fundamental principle: Technology belongs in service of humans, not humans in service of technology.

No personal data must become instrument of domination, control, or excessive concentration of power.

Governance must guarantee privacy protection, accountability of actors with data access, transparency toward users, balance between network security and individual freedom.

---

### Article 12 – Founding Commitment

Prove the existence of a human without unnecessarily exposing their identity.

Ensuring a real person participates in the network must never lead to sacrificing their dignity or privacy. HumChain builds infrastructure where trust relies on verified humans, but where humans always remain owners of their identity.

---

### Legal Compliance

HumChain adapts operation to applicable regulations. Legal analyses covering legal basis for biometric processing, data protection impact assessments, national restrictions on facial recognition, international data transfers, respective responsibilities of protocol / territorial representatives / KYC providers.

---

### Open Questions

- location of personal data hosting;
- data access modalities and control mechanisms;
- final system for non-custodial account recovery;
- specific measures in case of compromise of a biometric template;
- retention periods applicable to each data category per territorial regulatory requirements.

---

### Limitation of this Document

This document defines vision, principles, and founding commitments regarding data. It does not constitute a technical specification. Choices regarding IT architecture, cryptography used, storage systems, precise security mechanisms, software implementation belong to technical teams, blockchain engineers, and cybersecurity specialists.

HumChain Network considers that trust does not rely on absence of data, but on minimal, transparent, secure collection exclusively intended to protect human uniqueness, territorial sovereignty, and integrity of its global economic infrastructure.
