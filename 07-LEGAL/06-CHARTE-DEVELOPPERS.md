# Charter of Developers – HumChain Network

## Preamble

HumChain Network is an open global economic infrastructure whose mission is to allow each verified human being to participate in an economy based on trust, cooperation, and value creation in the real economy.

Developers play an essential role in this mission. They transform the founding principles of the protocol into technological solutions accessible to citizens, merchants, artisans, producers, businesses, and institutions.

This Charter defines values, responsibilities, and commitments expected from developers contributing to HumChain Network.

---

### Article 1 – Purpose of the Charter
This Charter establishes principles applicable to developers participating in development of HumChain Network or applications compatible with its infrastructure. It complements Constitution, White Paper, Yellow Paper, Terms of Use, and other official protocol documents.

### Article 2 – Mission of Developers
Design, maintain, and improve tools enabling functioning and adoption of HumChain Network:
- develop ecosystem applications;
- improve user experience;
- foster adoption of HCN in real economy;
- strengthen security, performance, and interoperability of developed solutions;
- encourage innovation serving communities.

### Article 3 – Fundamental Principles
Developers exercise missions respecting:
- human before technology;
- security by design;
- transparency;
- responsibility;
- quality;
- accessibility;
- inclusion.
Each technical evolution must serve mission of HumChain Network and respect users' rights.

### Article 4 – Respect of Founding Principles
Developers ensure contributions respect:
- one verified person owns single HumChain identity;
- no personal data is inscribed on blockchain;
- technology protects privacy;
- HCN remains currency serving real economy;
- HCN holders acquire no governance power solely from token holding;
- local economies constitute foundation of global economy developed by HumChain Network.

### Article 5 – Responsibility Towards Users
Developers recognize work may have direct impact on millions of users. Commit to: reliability of applications, simplicity of use, user security, reduction of error risks, continuous improvement of services. Take into consideration needs of populations with limited access to digital infrastructures.

### Article 6 – Cooperation
HumChain Network encourages open collaboration between developers, territorial representatives, validators, researchers, partners, and communities. This cooperation fosters innovation, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement of ecosystem. Contributions are guided by general interest of protocol rather than particular interests.

### Article 7 – Ethics
Developers commit not to use skills to create or voluntarily integrate functionalities contrary to values of HumChain Network. Avoid: mechanisms harming privacy, functionalities favoring discrimination, systems designed to circumvent Proof-of-Humanity principles, practices likely to compromise security or trust of users.

### Article 8 – Responsible Innovation
HumChain Network encourages experimentation and innovation. However, each innovation must be evaluated regarding impact on: network security, user protection, ecosystem stability, coherence with founding principles. Innovation has value only when it durably strengthens trust and usefulness of protocol.

### Article 9 – Commitment Declaration
By contributing to HumChain Network, each developer recognizes technology constitutes means and not end. Work aims to build tools allowing human beings to create, exchange, produce, cooperate, and develop local economies in open global environment. Developer thus contributes to building economic infrastructure serving humanity.

---

### Limitation of this Document
This Charter defines principles and responsibilities applicable to developers. It does not constitute technical documentation, software specification, or development guide. Architecture choices, programming languages, communication protocols, cryptographic mechanisms, and development procedures fall under technical teams. Role of founder is to set guiding principles future developments must respect.
