# Charter of Territorial Representatives – HumChain Network

## Preamble

HumChain Network is based on a fundamental conviction: a strong global economy cannot exist without strong local economies.

To support the development of the protocol in each territory, HumChain establishes a network of territorial representatives. Their mission is not to govern the protocol nor to exercise authority over users. They act as facilitators, educators, and catalysts for local economic development.

The territorial representative embodies HumChain Network's values in his community. He fosters trust, cooperation, and economic inclusion while respecting principles of neutrality, transparency, and responsibility.

---

### Article 1 – Purpose of the Charter
This Charter defines principles, responsibilities, rights, and duties of territorial representatives of HumChain Network. It complements the Constitution, Terms of Use, and other official protocol documents.

### Article 2 – Mission of the Territorial Representative
To foster adoption and development of HumChain Network in his territory:
- support territorial activation;
- inform population about objectives and functioning of HumChain Network;
- train new users;
- encourage use of HCN in real economy;
- promote integration of merchants, artisans, producers, businesses and institutions into ecosystem;
- contribute to development of strong, open and inclusive local economy.

### Article 3 – Principles of Action
- serve community before any personal interest;
- act with integrity, impartiality and transparency;
- respect dignity of each human being;
- promote equality among all verified humans;
- foster dialogue and cooperation between economic actors;
- faithfully represent values of HumChain Network.

### Article 4 – Limits of the Function
The territorial representative is neither owner of his territory, nor leader of HumChain Network. His function does not confer any right to: modify protocol rules on own initiative; control users' digital assets; access personal data without authorization; impose participation in network; grant privileges contrary to equality principles between users. The representative acts as facilitator serving his community.

### Article 5 – Relations with Community
Maintains close relationship with members of territory. Encourages listening to local needs, knowledge sharing, mutual aid between users, amicable resolution of difficulties, inclusion of populations often distant from digital services. Promotes culture of trust and responsibility.

### Article 6 – Appointment of Territorial Representative
Each activated territory has a territorial representative. Representative is appointed in accordance with rules defined by protocol and official documentation. Appointment constitutes service mission to community and creates no property right over territory concerned. Exercises functions as long as he respects principles and fulfills entrusted responsibilities.

### Article 7 – Territorial Organization
To foster proximity development, territorial representative may be assisted by regional, municipal, departmental, provincial or communal representatives, according to administrative organization specific to each country. Objectives: bring HumChain closer to populations, facilitate support for new users, support adoption by merchants and businesses, ensure better circulation of information, strengthen local economies. All local representatives exercise missions in respect of principles of this Charter.

### Article 8 – Ethical Commitments
Territorial representative commits to: act with honesty, impartiality and loyalty; respect all users without distinction of origin, nationality, culture, language or social condition; never use function to obtain undue personal advantage; preserve confidentiality of information to which he may have access; fight against any form of fraud or corruption; promote respectful and responsible behavior within community. Representative is ambassador of HumChain Network values.

### Article 9 – Duty of Neutrality
Representative exercises mission with neutrality. Cannot use function to promote personal political, religious, ideological or commercial interests. Ensures HumChain Network remains open economic infrastructure to all human beings, in respect of applicable laws and founding principles of protocol.

### Article 10 – Responsibility Towards Community
Acts in collective interest of territory. Encourages development of local economic exchanges, integration of new participants, economic and digital education, responsible use of HumChain ecosystem tools, cooperation between citizens, merchants, artisans, producers, businesses and institutions. Contributes to making territory active actor of global economy built by HumChain Network.

### Article 11 – Replacement of Representative
Function of territorial representative is neither permanent nor hereditary. When representative no longer fulfills obligations, acts contrary to HumChain Network principles, durably abandons mission or harms interests of community, he may be replaced in accordance with rules provided by protocol. Any replacement procedure must respect principles of transparency, fairness, adversarial process and general interest. Objective of replacement is to guarantee continuity of territorial development, not to sanction opinions or legitimate initiatives.

### Article 12 – Rights of Representative
In exercise of functions, territorial representative benefits from: access to official information necessary for mission; support by HumChain Network; training and educational resources; protection against pressures or discrimination related to exercise of mission, within limits provided by applicable laws. These rights exist to allow him to effectively accomplish mission serving community.

### Article 13 – Commitment Declaration
By accepting mission, each territorial representative commits to serve community with integrity, responsibility and transparency. Recognizes role consists of fostering economic development of territory, supporting users and promoting values of HumChain Network. Acts in interest of community, without seeking personal privilege, and contributes to building global economy based on human trust, cooperation between territories and value creation in real economy.

---

### Limitation of this Document
This Charter defines institutional principles applicable to territorial representatives. Operational modalities of appointment, mission monitoring, evaluation, internal organization and digital support tools will be defined later in functional documentation and operational procedures of HumChain Network.
