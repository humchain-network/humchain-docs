# HumChain Network - $HCN - Litepaper
**The First Equal-Territory Blockchain for Humanity**

#### Version 1.0 | October 2026

---

### 1. The Problem
L1s concentrate wealth and power in a few nations. The Global South has no economic sovereignty.

### 2. The Solution
HumChain: **22 Equal Territories.**
1 Territory = 1 Vote = 21,543,347 HCN.
No superpower. Equal infrastructure for all.

### 3. Tokenomics: $HCN
- **Ticker:** $HCN
- **Total Supply:** 473,933,649 HCN (Fixed Forever)
- **Allocation:** 21,543,347 HCN per Territory
- **Inflation:** 0% after Genesis. Deflationary via burn.

Distribution: 70% Community & Validation / 15% National Treasuries / 10% Foundation / 5% Liquidity.

### 4. Consensus
**Proof-of-Humanity + Territory.**
1 verified human = 1 validation power. Multi-territory quorum required. To attack the network, you must corrupt 15+ countries.

### 5. Roadmap: 7 Phases - Milestone-Based (No Dates)
We advance by economic maturity, not calendar.

**Phase I: Protocol Foundation**
Genesis of specs, consensus, tokenomics. The idea becomes law.

**Phase II: Local Economy Development**
Onboarding local validators, merchants, and builders in each of the 22 territories.

**Phase III: Currency Circulation**
$HCN begins to circulate as medium of exchange inside territories. Wallets, payments, P2P.

**Phase IV: Economic Activity Acceleration**
TVL growth, local marketplaces, acceleration of transactions and businesses on-chain.

**Phase V: Advanced Economic Infrastructures**
DeFi, bridges, SDKs, identity, credit, and treasury tools for territorial economies.

**Phase VI: Opening to the Global Economy**
Inter-territory trade, global CEX/DEX, cross-border settlement on HumChain.

**Phase VII: Mature Governance**
Full Sovereignty. Foundation steps back. DAO activated - 1 Territory = 1 Vote. Community owns the chain.

Full details in `05-ROADMAP/ROADMAP.md`

HumChain is not for trading. It is the economic infrastructure for a balanced humanity.
