# HumChain Network - $HCN - Litepaper
**The First Equal-Territory Blockchain for Humanity**

#### Version 1.0 | October 2026

---

### 1. The Problem
L1s concentrate wealth and power. HumChain equalizes it.

### 2. The Solution
One identical allocation per territory. No territory may receive a higher allocation.

### 3. Tokenomics: $HCN

**To ensure equality, each activation gives rise to a fixed allocation of: 473,933,649 HCN**

This allocation is distributed across four complementary components:

- **323,933,649 HCN** for community mining (Proof-of-Humanity)
- **25,000,000 HCN** for the National Treasury
- **50,000,000 HCN** for public sale and ecosystem access
- **75,000,000 HCN** for protocol development, validators, and merchant financing

**Global Supply = Number of Activated Territories x 473,933,649 HCN**
This distribution is defined by the Constitution of HumChain Network.

### 4. Consensus
Proof-of-Humanity + Territory. Multi-territory quorum. Unbribable.
