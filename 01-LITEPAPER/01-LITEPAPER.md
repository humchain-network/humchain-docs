### 1. The Problem

Today's digital economy remains highly concentrated.

Most blockchain ecosystems primarily facilitate the creation, transfer and trading of digital assets, while a large part of the real economy—citizens, merchants, artisans, producers and local businesses—remains insufficiently integrated.

Economic opportunities are often concentrated in a limited number of territories and among a small number of early participants.

---

### 2. The HumChain Vision

HumChain Network is a decentralized economic infrastructure built around human participation rather than capital concentration.

Its objective is to enable every territory to build its own local economy while remaining connected to a global economic network.

**Core Rule**

**1 Territory = 473,933,649 HCN = One Community = One Economy**

Every activated territory receives exactly the same allocation, ensuring equal economic opportunity across the network.

---

### 3. Human-Based Money

Before the activation of any territory:

**0 HCN exist.**

Each activated territory generates a fixed allocation of 473,933,649 HCN, distributed as follows:

- 323,933,649 HCN (68.35%) — Community Mining (Proof-of-Humanity)
- 25,000,000 HCN (5.27%) — National Treasury
- 50,000,000 HCN (10.55%) — Public Sale & Ecosystem Access
- 75,000,000 HCN (15.83%) — Protocol Development, Validators & Ecosystem

**Global Supply = Number of Activated Territories × 473,933,649 HCN**

This allocation is defined by the Constitution of HumChain Network.

---

### 4. Human Consensus

HumChain combines Proof-of-Humanity with territorial participation.

- One verified human = one mining unit.
- Mining distributes HCN to real people.
- Validation relies on decentralized territorial participation.
- The protocol is designed to strengthen trust, fairness and long-term network integrity.

---

### 5. Real Economy First

HumChain is designed for economic activity rather than speculation.

It enables the development of local and global commerce through applications such as digital payments, marketplaces and future economic services built on top of the protocol.

In accordance with its Commercial Neutrality Principle, HumChain Network is neither a centralized exchange (CEX) nor a decentralized exchange (DEX). Trading, when it exists, takes place on independent external platforms and is not part of the protocol itself.

### 6. Roadmap (2026–2030)

**Phase 0 — Foundation (2026)**

Founding documents, global community, representatives, identity and trust infrastructure.

**Phase 1 — Human Network (2026–2027)**

Formation of territorial communities and onboarding of the first verified humans.

**Phase 2 — Economic Activation (2027)**

Activation of the first territories, creation of territorial allocations and beginning of community mining.

**Phase 3 — Birth of the Economy (2027–2028)**

Launch of HumWallet, HumPay, merchant onboarding and first commercial activity.

**Phase 4 — Expansion (2028–2030)**

Interconnection of territories, continental expansion and ecosystem growth.

**Phase 5 — Global Economic Infrastructure (After 2030)**

Decentralized governance, open ecosystem and long-term transmission of the network.
