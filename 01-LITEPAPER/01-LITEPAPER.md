### 1. The Problem
Current L1s concentrate wealth and power in a few territories.
Early insiders, VCs, and a few regions capture everything.
HumChain equalizes it from day one.

### 2. The Solution
One identical allocation per territory.
No territory may receive a higher allocation than another.

**Core Rule:** 1 Territory = 473,933,649 HCN = One Community = One Economy.

### 3. Tokenomics: HCN

Before any territory is activated: **0 HCN**.

Each activation generates a fixed allocation of **473,933,649 HCN**, distributed as:

- **323,933,649 HCN (68.35%)** — Community Mining (Proof-of-Humanity)
- **25,000,000 HCN (5.27%)** — National Treasury
- **50,000,000 HCN (10.55%)** — Public Sale & Ecosystem Access
- **75,000,000 HCN (15.83%)** — Protocol Development, Validators & Merchant Financing

**Global Supply = Number of Activated Territories x 473,933,649 HCN**

This distribution is defined by the Constitution of HumChain Network and cannot be changed.

### 4. Consensus
Proof-of-Humanity + Territory.
One verified human = one mining unit.
Multi-territory quorum required to validate.
Unbribable.

### 5. Roadmap 2026-2030

HumChain follows a different progression: Community first. Infrastructure second. Real economy last.

**Phase 0 — Foundation (2026)**
Finalization of founding documents, global community building, representatives onboarding, identity & trust tools.

**Phase 1 — Human Network (2026-2027)**
Territorial communities development, representatives training, first circle of verified users.

**Phase 2 — Economic Activation (2027)**
First territories activation. Generation of 473,933,649 HCN per territory. Start of community mining and national treasuries deployment.

**Phase 3 — Birth of HumChain Economy (2027-2028)**
Launch of HumWallet & HumPay. Merchant integration. First economic exchanges.

**Phase 4 — Expansion & Interconnection (2028-2030)**
Multi-territory deployment, local economies interconnection, continental expansion.

**Phase 5 — World Economic Infrastructure (After 2030)**
Mature governance: 1 Territory = 1 Vote. Open ecosystem for developers. Transmission to future generations.
