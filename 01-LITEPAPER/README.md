### 1. The Problem

Today's digital economies often concentrate opportunities, wealth and influence in a limited number of territories and among early participants.

Many individuals, merchants, artisans, producers and local economies remain disconnected from global digital infrastructure.

HumChain Network addresses this imbalance by placing human participation and territorial equality at the foundation of the economy.

---

### 2. The Solution

HumChain introduces a new model: an economic infrastructure where every territory starts with the same potential.

No territory receives a greater allocation than another.

**Core Rule:**
**1 Territory = 473,933,649 HCN = One Community = One Economy**

Each territory develops its own economic activity while remaining connected to a global human network.

---

### 3. Tokenomics: HCN

Before any territory is activated:

**0 HCN exist.**

Each activation generates a fixed allocation of:

**473,933,649 HCN**

Distributed as:

- 323,933,649 HCN (68.35%) — Community Mining (Proof-of-Humanity)
- 25,000,000 HCN (5.27%) — National Treasury
- 50,000,000 HCN (10.55%) — Public Sale & Ecosystem Access
- 75,000,000 HCN (15.83%) — Protocol Development, Validators & Ecosystem Support

**Global Supply = Number of Activated Territories × 473,933,649 HCN**

This distribution is defined by the Constitution of HumChain Network.

---

### 4. Consensus

HumChain combines:

**Proof-of-Humanity + Territory**

Principles:

- One verified human = one mining unit.
- Human participation is the foundation of currency creation.
- Territorial decentralization strengthens network neutrality.
- Validation requires multi-territory participation.

---

### 5. The Economic Model

HumChain follows a different progression:

**Community First. Infrastructure Second. Real Economy Finally.**

The protocol provides the foundation for economic applications connecting citizens, merchants, producers, companies and institutions.

HumChain is designed for economic activity, not only digital asset transfer.

According to its Commercial Neutrality Principle:

**HumChain Network is neither a Centralized Exchange (CEX) nor a Decentralized Exchange (DEX).**

Trading is an external service provided by independent platforms. HumChain itself remains a decentralized economic infrastructure.

### 6. Roadmap 2026-2030

**Phase 0 — Foundation (2026)**
Finalization of founding documents, global community building, representatives onboarding, identity and trust tools.

**Phase 1 — Human Network (2026-2027)**
Development of territorial communities, representative training and first verified users.

**Phase 2 — Economic Activation (2027)**
Activation of the first territories, generation of territorial allocations, beginning of community mining and deployment of national treasuries.

**Phase 3 — Birth of HumChain Economy (2027-2028)**
Launch of HumWallet and HumPay, merchant integration and first economic activities.

**Phase 4 — Expansion & Interconnection (2028-2030)**
Connection between territories, local economy development and continental expansion.

**Phase 5 — World Economic Infrastructure (After 2030)**
Mature governance, open ecosystem for developers and transmission of the network to future generations.
