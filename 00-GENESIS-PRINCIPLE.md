# GENESIS PRINCIPLE - HumChain Network

**The territory does not pre-exist. It is activated.**

HumChain does not start with a list of 246 countries.
At Genesis, `activated_territories = []`. The chain is empty.

### The Activation Rule
The first human who completes a valid Proof-of-Humanity in a territory CREATES that territory inside HumChain.

That activation automatically mints ONE identical allocation of:
**473,933,649 HCN**

As defined in the Economic Constitution:
- 323,933,649 HCN for community mining (Proof-of-Humanity)
- 25,000,000 HCN for the National Treasury
- 50,000,000 HCN for public sale and ecosystem access
- 75,000,000 HCN for protocol development, validators, and merchant financing

### The Law
`Total Supply = N_activated_territories * 473,933,649 HCN`

- No pre-mine
- No waiting list
- No territory may receive a higher allocation

A territory exists only when a human activates it.
