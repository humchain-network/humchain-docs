# TITLE IX — IMMUTABLE CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES

### Article 95 — Object of Immutable Principles
The principles set forth in this Title constitute the permanent foundations of HumChain Network.
They define the identity of the protocol and guarantee its continuity over time.
No technical, economic, institutional, or organizational evolution may contradict them without a constitutional revision in accordance with the provisions of this Constitution.

### Article 96 — The Founding Principle
The fundamental principle of HumChain Network is:
"No human, no money. Genesis = 0."
At the genesis of the protocol:
- no HCN exists;
- no territory has an allocation;
- no monetary creation is possible.
Any monetary creation is exclusively conditioned on the activation of a territory by its first verified inhabitant.
This principle constitutes the origin of the entire economic architecture of HumChain Network.

### Article 97 — Proof of Humanity
HumChain Network is based on proof of humanity.
Each natural person may have only one personal account.
The principle:
"One person. One face. One account."
guarantees fairness of monetary distribution, trust between participants, and integrity of the protocol.
No evolution may remove or weaken this principle.

### Article 98 — Primacy of the Real Economy
HumChain Network recognizes that wealth is created by human activity.
Money constitutes an instrument for circulation of this wealth.
The protocol is designed to support commerce, production, entrepreneurship, services, innovation, and cooperation.
No evolution may transform HumChain Network into an infrastructure whose primary purpose is speculation.

### Article 99 — The Territorial Principle
Each activated territory constitutes an autonomous local economy integrated into a global infrastructure.
The development of HumChain Network is based on cooperation between territories.
The protocol affirms that a sustainable global economy can only be built from strong local economies.

### Article 100 — Human Decentralization
The decentralization of HumChain Network rests above all on people, communities, and territories.
No individual, company, validator, institution, or technical authority may exercise exclusive control over the protocol.
Power is distributed among actors in accordance with the provisions of this Constitution.

### Article 101 — Protection of the Currency
The rules relating to creation, distribution, allocation, and destruction of HCN constitute essential guarantees of economic stability of the protocol.
No monetary issuance may be made outside the mechanisms provided for by this Constitution.
Any modification of the fundamental rules of the currency falls exclusively under the constitutional revision procedure.

### Article 102 — Continuity of the Mission
HumChain Network has the permanent mission of building a global economic infrastructure by strengthening local economies.
This mission constitutes the identity of the protocol.
Any evolution must contribute to its realization.
No decision may divert HumChain Network from this purpose.

### Article 103 — Transmission to Future Generations
This Constitution is established to ensure the continuity of HumChain Network beyond its founders and the generations that created it.
Each generation receives the responsibility to preserve fundamental principles, improve the protocol, and transmit this heritage to subsequent generations.
Technology may evolve.
Services may evolve.
Uses may evolve.
But the founding principles will remain the permanent reference of HumChain Network.

### Article 104 — Constitutional Protection Clause
The principles set forth in this Title constitute the constitutional core of HumChain Network.
Any revision proposal undermining these principles is deemed contrary to the identity of the protocol.
In case of doubt, any interpretation must favor the solution most consistent with:
- proof of humanity;
- primacy of the real economy;
- human decentralization;
- development of local economies;
- founding mission of HumChain Network.

### Article 105 — Constitutional Heritage
The Constitution of HumChain Network is not only a set of rules.
It constitutes the expression of a vision.
A vision in which money is born from humans.
A vision in which each territory builds its own economy before contributing to the global economy.
A vision in which technology remains at the service of trust, cooperation, and sustainable development.
This vision is the heritage that HumChain Network intends to transmit to all future generations.
