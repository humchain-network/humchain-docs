# TITLE VIII — PROTOCOL GOVERNANCE

### Article 84 — Principle of Governance
The governance of HumChain Network guarantees the evolution of the protocol in compliance with its founding mission.
It ensures balance between innovation, stability, transparency, and protection of constitutional principles.
No governance decision may be contrary to this Constitution.

### Article 85 — Mission of Governance
Governance has in particular the mission to:
- preserve the identity of HumChain Network;
- support the evolution of the protocol;
- guarantee transparency of decisions;
- ensure community participation;
- protect the interests of activated territories;
- promote sustainable development of the ecosystem.
Any decision must be taken in the general interest of the protocol.

### Article 86 — Actors of Governance
Governance is exercised in a decentralized manner by actors recognized by the protocol, including:
- verified users;
- merchants;
- validators;
- developers;
- representatives of territories;
- activated territories.
The modalities of participation and voting are defined by governance rules, in compliance with this Constitution.

### Article 87 — Transparency of Decisions
Any proposal submitted to governance must be made public.
It specifies in particular:
- its objective;
- its technical consequences;
- its economic effects;
- its impact on users and territories.
Debates, proposals, voting results, and final decisions are preserved to guarantee their traceability.

### Article 88 — Evolution of the Protocol
HumChain Network is designed to evolve.
Governance may propose improvements concerning:
- technical performance;
- ecosystem services;
- economic tools;
- operational mechanisms.
Any evolution must strengthen the mission of the protocol and may not call into question protected constitutional principles.

### Article 89 — Protection of Fundamental Principles
The following principles constitute the permanent foundation of HumChain Network:
- "No human, no money. Genesis = 0.";
- monetary creation conditioned on activation of a territory;
- initial global supply set at 0 HCN;
- maximum allocation of 473,933,649 HCN per activated territory;
- primacy of the real economy;
- human decentralization.
These principles may only be modified according to the constitutional revision procedure provided in this text.

### Article 90 — Normative Hierarchy
The fundamental documents of HumChain Network are hierarchized as follows:
1. The Constitution;
2. The White Paper;
3. The Yellow Paper;
4. Governance decisions compatible with the preceding texts.
In case of contradiction, the higher-ranking document prevails.

### Article 91 — Community Participation
Governance is based on active participation of the community.
Each participant called to intervene in governance exercises its rights with responsibility, transparency, and in the interest of the protocol.
Governance does not constitute an instrument of individual power but a mechanism for protecting the collective interest.

### Article 92 — Protection Against Abuse
Any attempt to divert governance for the benefit of particular interests, to manipulate decision-making mechanisms, or to undermine the institutional integrity of HumChain Network constitutes a violation of this Constitution.
The protocol provides the necessary mechanisms to prevent, detect, and sanction such behavior.

### Article 93 — Institutional Continuity
Governance guarantees the continuity of HumChain Network.
No change of leader, representative, validator, or generation may interrupt the operation of the protocol or call into question its mission.
The founding vision is transmitted to future generations through this Constitution.

### Article 94 — Purpose of Governance
The governance of HumChain Network has the sole purpose of preserving a global economic infrastructure based on proof of humanity, cooperation, responsibility, and transparency.
It ensures that each evolution serves the real economy, strengthens local economies, and respects the dignity of each human being.
Thus, governance protects not only a computer protocol, but also the vision that gives it meaning.
