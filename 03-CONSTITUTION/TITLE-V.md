# TITLE V — MERCHANTS AND SHOPS

### Article 49 — Role of Merchants
Merchants, artisans, producers, farmers, entrepreneurs, service providers, and businesses constitute the engine of real economy of HumChain Network. Through their activities, they create value, favor circulation of HCN, stimulate exchanges between territories, and strengthen utility of infrastructure. Protocol recognizes commerce as main mechanism of sustainable value creation. In accordance with founding principle of HumChain Network: Human creates supply. Mining distributes money. Commerce creates value.

### Article 50 — Merchant Status
Any user holder of merchant account may exercise economic activity within HumChain Network. This status allows notably: opening one or several digital shops; commercializing goods and services; using economic services of protocol; accessing professional tools of ecosystem. Merchant remains legally responsible for his activity and acts in respect of applicable laws as well as provisions of this Constitution.

### Article 51 — Creation of a Shop
A shop constitutes digital economic identity of a merchant on HumChain Network. Its creation requires notably: an active merchant account; attachment to an activated territory; information allowing identification of exercised activity; acceptance of protocol rules. Each shop benefits from economic infrastructure of HumChain Network as well as security ensured by national treasury of territory to which it is attached.

### Article 52 — Mandatory Information
In order to strengthen trust between economic actors, each shop must provide information necessary for its identification. This information includes notably: territory of activity; city or commune; neighborhood or address when it exists; professional contact details; category of activity; presentation of products or services offered. Protocol may provide complementary information when necessary for proper functioning of ecosystem.

### Article 53 — Territorial Development of Commerce
Commercial development follows progression based on activated territories. Economic activities may evolve according to three levels: 1. Local and national level, within origin territory; 2. Continental level, between neighboring activated territories; 3. Global level, between all territories integrated into network. This progression guarantees that global economy rests on solid and really active local economies.

### Article 54 — Economic Obligations
Exploitation of a shop implies respect of obligations provided by protocol. These include notably: payment of applicable economic rents; respect of deposit mechanisms when they exist; compliant use of economic services of HumChain Network. These mechanisms participate in sustainable financing of infrastructure, security of network, and economic stability of HCN.

### Article 55 — Economic Rents
Rents constitute essential resource of protocol. They finance simultaneously: progressive reduction of monetary supply; security of activated territories; operation of network. Their distribution is fixed as follows: 50% are definitively destroyed (burn); 25% feed national treasury of concerned territory; 25% remunerate validators and operation of network. This distribution can only be modified by means of constitutional revision.

### Article 56 — Commercial Deposits
Protocol may provide deposit mechanisms for certain activities requiring economic guarantee. Deposit protects users, strengthens trust between parties, and contributes to security of exchanges. Modalities relating to its deposit, use, restitution, or retention are defined by economic rules of protocol.

### Article 57 — Freedom to Undertake
HumChain Network guarantees freedom to undertake. Each merchant remains free to: define his prices; choose his partners; organize his activity; develop his products and services. Protocol provides common economic infrastructure without intervening in management of businesses that use it.

### Article 58 — Economic Promotion
Merchants may use promotion services offered by HumChain Network, notably HumAds. These services allow promoting products, services, events, or economic activities to users of network. All payments related to these services are made in HCN, thus strengthening economic utility of native currency.

### Article 59 — Commercial Payments
Merchants may receive payments by means of solutions offered by HumPay. Protocol facilitates exchanges between individuals, merchants, and businesses, both at local level and between activated territories. International payments may be developed in accordance with technical and economic specifications of network.

### Article 60 — Commercial Liability
Each merchant remains solely responsible for: quality of goods and services offered; respect of his commitments toward clients; compliance of his activity with applicable laws. HumChain Network provides economic infrastructure but is not party to contracts concluded between users.

### Article 61 — Contribution to Mission of HumChain Network
Merchants occupy essential place in economic balance of protocol. Through their activities, they: create real value; strengthen circulation of HCN; develop local economies; favor adoption of network services; participate in progressive construction of interconnected global economy. Commerce thus constitutes natural link between monetary creation based on proof of humanity and sustainable prosperity of ecosystem.
