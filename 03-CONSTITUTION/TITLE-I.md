# TITLE I — FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES

### Article 1 — Purpose of the Constitution

This Constitution constitutes the supreme norm of HumChain Network.

It defines the legal, economic, institutional, and philosophical principles that govern the protocol, its participants, its economic mechanisms, and its evolution.

Any rule, decision, technical, economic, organizational, or institutional evolution must be compliant with its provisions.

---

### Article 2 — Mission of the Protocol

HumChain Network's mission is to build a global economic infrastructure based on strengthening local economies.

The protocol allows citizens, merchants, artisans, producers, entrepreneurs, businesses, developers, validators, and territories to participate in an open, decentralized, sustainable digital economy based on proof of humanity.

Its purpose is to progressively connect local economies in order to build a more accessible, more cooperative, and more resilient global economy.

---

### Article 3 — Primacy of the Real Economy

HumChain Network recognizes that all wealth comes from human activity.

Work, production, trade, services, innovation, agriculture, craftsmanship, industry, and entrepreneurship constitute the true sources of value creation.

Money is not intended to create this wealth; it facilitates its circulation.

The protocol is designed to support the real economy before any speculative logic.

---

### Article 4 — Fundamental Principle of Monetary Creation

HumChain Network rests on the founding principle:

"No human, no money. Genesis = 0."

At the genesis of the protocol, no HCN exists.

No territory possesses a monetary allocation.

Monetary creation can only occur after the activation of a territory by its first verified inhabitant in accordance with the rules of the protocol.

Thus, any issuance of HCN results directly from real human presence.

---

### Article 5 — Territorial Activation

Each territory recognized by HumChain Network becomes a digital economy only when it is activated by its first verified inhabitant.

This activation automatically triggers the creation of the constitutional allocation of 473,933,649 HCN, distributed in accordance with the provisions of Title II.

The activation of a territory is definitive.

It confers no ownership right over the territory, but marks its official entry into the HumChain Network ecosystem.

---

### Article 6 — Human Decentralization

HumChain Network is a decentralized infrastructure.

Its decentralization rests on real people, activated territories, and distributed governance.

No natural person, company, organization, institution, or technical authority can exercise exclusive control over the protocol.

Decentralization constitutes a permanent principle of its operation.

---

### Article 7 — Equality of Participants

All participants are equal before the rules of the protocol.

No distinction can be based on nationality, origin, place of residence, profession, economic situation, gender, or any other consideration foreign to the provisions of this Constitution.

Rights and obligations result exclusively from constitutional rules and governance mechanisms.

---

### Article 8 — Principle of Proof of Humanity

HumChain Network applies the principle:

"One person. One face. One account."

Each natural person may only have a single personal account.

Identity verification mechanisms aim to guarantee equitable distribution of currency, protect the integrity of the protocol, and preserve trust between participants.

---

### Article 9 — Responsibility of Participants

Each participant is responsible for:

- the accuracy of the information they provide;
- the security of their account;
- compliance with the rules of the protocol;
- fair use of HumChain Network services.

Any fraud, identity theft, creation of multiple accounts, manipulation, or attempt to circumvent security mechanisms may result in sanctions provided for by this Constitution.

---

### Article 10 — Transparency

The fundamental rules of HumChain Network are public, verifiable, and applicable identically to all.

Monetary creation, distribution of territorial allocations, economic mechanisms, governance rules, as well as operations of national treasuries must be able to be controlled by the community in accordance with protocol specifications.

Transparency constitutes a permanent principle of governance.

---

### Article 11 — Responsible Innovation

Any innovation developed within HumChain Network must contribute to the development of the real economy.

Applications, services, infrastructures, and technical evolutions must produce concrete utility for users, merchants, businesses, and activated territories.

Innovation can never call into question the fundamental principles enshrined in this Constitution.

---

### Article 12 — Cooperation Between Territories

Each activated territory constitutes an autonomous local economy integrated into the global infrastructure of HumChain Network.

Territories are called upon to cooperate in order to progressively develop commercial exchanges, services, investments, and common initiatives.

The global economy is built through cooperation between strong local economies.

---

### Article 13 — Protection of the Founding Vision

The founding mission of HumChain Network constitutes the permanent foundation of the protocol.

Any evolution must preserve:

- the principle "No human, no money. Genesis = 0.";
- monetary creation conditioned by human activation;
- strengthening of local economies;
- primacy of the real economy;
- human decentralization;
- transparency and responsibility of governance.

No evolution can divert HumChain Network from its mission without following the constitutional revision procedure.

---

### Article 14 — Interpretation of the Constitution

The provisions of this Constitution are interpreted in accordance with the following objectives:

- protect proof of humanity;
- preserve the economic stability of the protocol;
- guarantee transparent and responsible governance;
- strengthen local economies;
- foster an open and cooperative global economy;
- ensure continuity of the founding vision of HumChain Network.

In case of doubt, any interpretation must favor the solution most compliant with the mission and fundamental principles of the protocol.
