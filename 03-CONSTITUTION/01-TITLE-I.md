# TITLE I — FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES

### Article 1 — Purpose of the Constitution

This Constitution constitutes the supreme normative foundation of HumChain Network.

It defines the constitutional, economic, institutional, and philosophical principles that govern the protocol, its participants, its monetary mechanisms, its governance, and its evolution.

Any technical, economic, organizational, or institutional evolution of HumChain Network must remain compliant with the provisions established by this Constitution.

---

### Article 2 — Mission of the Protocol

HumChain Network's mission is to build a decentralized global economic infrastructure by strengthening local economies.

The protocol enables citizens, merchants, artisans, producers, entrepreneurs, businesses, developers, validators, and territories to participate in an open economic network based on Proof-of-Humanity and territorial participation.

Its purpose is to progressively connect local economies while preserving their autonomy, identity, and ability to create value.

---

### Article 3 — Primacy of the Real Economy

HumChain Network recognizes that economic value originates from human activity.

Work, production, commerce, services, innovation, agriculture, craftsmanship, industry, and entrepreneurship are fundamental sources of value creation.

Money does not create economic value by itself; it facilitates exchange, coordination, and circulation of value created by humans.

The protocol is designed to support the real economy before speculative activity.

---

### Article 4 — Fundamental Principle of Monetary Creation

HumChain Network is founded on the principle:

**"No Human, No Money. Genesis = 0."**

At the genesis of the protocol:

- no HCN exists;
- no territory has an economic allocation;
- no monetary supply is pre-created.

The creation of HCN can only occur after the activation of a territory by its first verified human participant according to the rules defined by this Constitution.

Therefore, monetary creation is directly linked to verified human presence.

---

### Article 5 — Territorial Activation

Each territory recognized by HumChain Network becomes an active economic territory only after activation by its first verified inhabitant.

This activation triggers the creation of the constitutional allocation of:

**473,933,649 HCN**

distributed according to the rules established in Title II.

Territorial activation grants no ownership right over the territory.

It represents the official integration of that territory into the HumChain Network ecosystem.

---

### Article 6 — Human Decentralization

HumChain Network is a decentralized economic infrastructure.

Its decentralization is based on:

- verified human participation;
- activated territories;
- distributed responsibilities;
- decentralized governance.

No individual, company, institution, organization, or technical authority may obtain exclusive control over the protocol.

Human decentralization is a permanent principle of HumChain Network.

---

### Article 7 — Equality of Participants

All participants are equal before the rules of the protocol
