# TITLE VI — VALIDATORS AND NETWORK SECURITY

### Article 62 — Mission of Validators
Validators ensure the technical security, availability, and integrity of HumChain Network.
They participate in particular in block validation, transaction verification, execution of protocol rules, and maintenance of network consensus.
They exercise their mission in the collective interest of the ecosystem and may not act contrary to the principles defined by this Constitution.
Their role is to protect the infrastructure, not to exercise control over it.

### Article 63 — Neutrality of Validators
Validators exercise their functions with impartiality, independence, and neutrality.
They may not, in particular:
- favor a user, merchant, or territory;
- censor a transaction compliant with protocol rules;
- use their position to obtain undue economic advantage;
- undermine equality between participants.
Any operation compliant with the protocol must be treated according to the same rules, regardless of territory or actors concerned.

### Article 64 — Eligibility Conditions
The technical specifications of the protocol determine the necessary conditions to become a validator.
Any candidate must in particular:
- maintain infrastructure compliant with network requirements;
- actively contribute to protocol security;
- respect transparency rules;
- accept the control mechanisms provided by governance.
The quality of validator constitutes a responsibility in service of the entire community.

### Article 65 — Obligations of Validators
Validators are required to:
- maintain their operational infrastructures;
- faithfully apply protocol rules;
- guarantee the integrity of validated transactions;
- contribute to network continuity;
- report any anomaly likely to affect the security or stability of HumChain Network.
Any serious negligence or any behavior deliberately contrary to the protocol may result in sanctions provided by governance.

### Article 66 — Financing of Validators
Validators are remunerated exclusively through the economic mechanisms provided for by this Constitution.
Their financing is based in particular on:
- a portion of the 75,000,000 HCN allocation created upon activation of each territory and intended for protocol development, validators, and merchant financing;
- the share of economic revenues attributed to them in accordance with protocol rules, including a fraction of rents and applicable fees.
No additional monetary issuance may be created to remunerate validators.

### Article 67 — National Treasuries and Territorial Security
Each activated territory has a national treasury endowed with 25,000,000 HCN.
Its primary mission is to contribute to the security and resilience of the local network.
It may in particular finance:
- infrastructure necessary for the functioning of the territory;
- initiatives strengthening network security;
- continuity of essential services;
- needs validated by territorial governance.
**Fundamental Principle:**
No verified human, no activation.
No activation, no treasury.
No treasury, no territorial security.

### Article 68 — Liability of Validators
Each validator is liable for operations carried out within the framework of its functions.
Any manipulation, fraudulent collusion, data falsification, serious negligence, or abusive use of its status may result in:
- suspension of its functions;
- withdrawal of its validator status;
- any other sanction provided by governance rules.

### Article 69 — Collective Security
The security of HumChain Network rests on shared responsibility.
It results from the coordinated contribution of:
- validators, who ensure technical security;
- developers, who improve the protocol;
- users, who guarantee proof of humanity;
- merchants, who create economic activity;
- national treasuries, which strengthen territorial resilience.
No single actor can ensure the security of the network alone.

### Article 70 — Resilience of the Protocol
HumChain Network is designed to maintain its operation despite failures that may affect certain actors or infrastructures.
Its resilience is based in particular on:
- decentralization of validators;
- autonomy of activated territories;
- national treasuries;
- diversity of economic actors;
- decentralized governance.
A non-activated territory does not constitute a weakness of the protocol, since no economy, no currency, and no local infrastructure yet exist there.

### Article 71 — Protection of Fundamental Rules
No validator, group of validators, or technical actor may unilaterally modify the fundamental principles of HumChain Network.
In particular, the following are protected:
- the principle "No human, no money. Genesis = 0";
- territorial activation by a verified human;
- the constitutional allocation of 473,933,649 HCN per activated territory;
- the distribution of territorial allocations;
- the fundamental mechanisms of monetary creation, distribution, and destruction.
Any evolution must respect the constitutional revision procedure.

### Article 72 — Protection of Infrastructure
Any attempt to compromise the security, availability, or integrity of HumChain Network constitutes an attack on the protocol.
The network implements permanent mechanisms for prevention, detection, and incident response, in accordance with technical specifications and constitutional principles.
The protection of HumChain Network is based on the combination of technology, governance, validators, national treasuries, and the responsibility of the entire community.
