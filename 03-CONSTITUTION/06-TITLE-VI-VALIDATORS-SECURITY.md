# TITLE VI — VALIDATORS AND NETWORK SECURITY

### Article 62 — Mission of Validators

Validators ensure the technical integrity, availability, and security of HumChain Network.

They participate in the operation of the protocol, including transaction validation, consensus mechanisms, and enforcement of protocol rules according to technical specifications.

Validators exercise their mission in the collective interest of the network.

Their role is to protect the infrastructure, not to control it.

---

### Article 63 — Neutrality of Validators

Validators must perform their functions with impartiality, independence, and neutrality.

They may not:

- favor a user, merchant, application, or territory;
- censor valid protocol operations;
- exploit their position for unfair economic advantage;
- undermine equality between participants.

All compliant protocol operations must be treated according to the same rules.

---

### Article 64 — Eligibility Conditions

Technical specifications of HumChain Network define the conditions required to become a validator.

Candidates must:

- operate infrastructure compatible with protocol requirements;
- contribute to network security;
- respect transparency obligations;
- accept monitoring and accountability mechanisms established by governance.

Validator status represents a responsibility toward the entire network.

---

### Article 65 — Obligations of Validators

Validators must:

- maintain operational infrastructure;
- apply protocol rules faithfully;
- preserve transaction integrity;
- contribute to network continuity;
- report security or operational anomalies.

Intentional misconduct, severe negligence, or actions contrary to protocol integrity may result in sanctions defined by governance.

---

### Article 66 — Financing of Validators

Validators are compensated through economic mechanisms established by this Constitution and protocol rules.

Their support may include:

- allocations reserved for protocol development and validator ecosystem support;
- economic revenues assigned according to protocol mechanisms.

No additional HCN creation may occur solely for validator compensation.

Validator incentives must preserve monetary integrity.

---

### Article 67 — National Treasuries and Territorial Resilience

Each activated territory has a National Treasury allocated with:

**25,000,000 HCN**

Its primary mission is to strengthen territorial resilience and support the sustainable operation of the local ecosystem.

It may contribute to:

- infrastructure development;
- continuity of essential services;
- initiatives supporting network resilience;
- territorial economic development according to governance rules.

Fundamental Principle:

**No verified human, no activation.
No activation, no treasury.
No treasury, no territorial resilience.**

---

### Article 68 — Liability of Validators

Each validator is responsible for operations performed within the scope of its functions.

The following may result in sanctions:

- manipulation of protocol operations;
- fraudulent coordination;
- falsification of information;
- abusive use of validator status;
- serious negligence affecting network integrity.

Sanctions are determined according to governance procedures.
