# TITLE V — MERCHANTS AND SHOPS

### Article 49 — Role of Merchants

Merchants, artisans, producers, farmers, entrepreneurs, service providers, and businesses constitute essential participants in the real economy of HumChain Network.

Through their activities, they create economic value, strengthen exchanges, increase the utility of HCN, and contribute to the development of local and interconnected economies.

HumChain Network recognizes commerce as a fundamental mechanism for transforming human activity into economic utility.

Founding principle:

**Human creates economic activity. Mining distributes currency. Commerce creates utility.**

---

### Article 50 — Merchant Status

Any user holding a Merchant Account may participate in economic activities within HumChain Network.

This status allows participants to:

- create economic profiles;
- present goods and services;
- use merchant-oriented ecosystem tools;
- participate in economic exchanges.

Merchants remain independent economic actors and are responsible for their activities according to applicable laws and regulations.

---

### Article 51 — Creation of a Shop

A shop represents the digital economic identity of a merchant within the decentralized infrastructure of HumChain Network.

Its creation requires:

- an active Merchant Account;
- attachment to an activated territory;
- information describing the economic activity;
- acceptance of protocol rules.

A shop benefits from the economic infrastructure provided by HumChain Network while remaining independently managed by its owner.

---

### Article 52 — Mandatory Information

To strengthen trust between economic participants, each shop must provide information necessary for identification and transparency.

This information may include:

- territory of activity;
- city or local area;
- professional contact information;
- category of activity;
- description of products or services offered.

Additional information may be required according to protocol needs.

---

### Article 53 — Territorial Development of Commerce

Economic activity within HumChain Network develops progressively according to territorial integration.

Commerce may evolve through three levels:

1. Local and national activity within the origin territory;
2. Continental exchanges between activated territories;
3. Global economic connections between territories integrated into the network.

A global economy is built through strong and active local economies.

---

### Article 54 — Economic Obligations

The operation of economic activities may require compliance with protocol-defined mechanisms.

These may include:

- applicable economic contributions;
- deposit mechanisms when required;
- responsible use of ecosystem services.

Such mechanisms contribute to infrastructure sustainability, network security and economic stability.

---

### Article 55 — Economic Rents

Economic rents may constitute one of the mechanisms supporting HumChain Network infrastructure.

Their purposes may include:

- supporting monetary management mechanisms;
- contributing to territorial resilience;
- supporting network operation and security.

Distribution rules are defined by economic governance mechanisms and must remain compliant with this Constitution.

---

### Article 56 — Commercial Deposits

HumChain Network may introduce deposit mechanisms for specific economic activities requiring additional guarantees.

These deposits aim to:

- strengthen trust between participants;
- protect economic interactions;
- improve reliability of commercial activity.

Conditions relating to deposit creation, use and restitution are defined by protocol economic rules.

---

### Article 57 — Freedom to Undertake

HumChain Network guarantees freedom of economic initiative.

Each merchant remains free to:

- define prices;
- choose partners;
- organize activities;
- develop products and services.

The protocol provides infrastructure and tools but does not manage businesses operating within the ecosystem.

---

### Article 58 — Economic Promotion

Merchants may access promotion services provided by ecosystem applications, including HumAds.

These services allow economic actors to increase visibility of their products, services, events, or activities.

Such services contribute to the economic utility of HCN while respecting the decentralized nature of the infrastructure.

---

### Article 59 — Commercial Payments

Merchants may receive payments through payment solutions developed within the HumChain ecosystem, including HumPay.

These tools facilitate economic exchanges between individuals, merchants and businesses.

HumChain Network provides payment infrastructure but does not act as a commercial intermediary or exchange platform.

---

### Article 60 — Commercial Liability

Each merchant remains solely responsible for:

- quality of goods and services;
- commitments made to customers;
- compliance with applicable laws.

HumChain Network provides economic infrastructure but is not a party to commercial agreements between participants.

---

### Article 61 — Contribution to the Mission of HumChain Network

Merchants play an essential role in the economic balance of HumChain Network.

Through their activities, they:

- create real economic value;
- strengthen HCN utility;
- develop local economies;
- encourage adoption of economic infrastructure;
- contribute to the connection of territories.

Commerce represents the natural bridge between human-based monetary creation and sustainable economic activity.

**HumChain Network supports commerce.**

**It does not replace it.**
