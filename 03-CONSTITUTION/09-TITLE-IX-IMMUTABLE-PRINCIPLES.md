# TITLE IX — IMMUTABLE CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES

### Article 91 — Object of Immutable Principles

The principles established in this Title constitute the permanent foundation of HumChain Network.

They define the identity, purpose, and continuity of the protocol.

No technical, economic, institutional, or organizational evolution may contradict these principles except through the constitutional revision procedure defined by this Constitution.

---

### Article 92 — The Founding Principle

The fundamental principle of HumChain Network is:

**"No Human, No Money. Genesis = 0."**

At the genesis of the protocol:

- no HCN exists;
- no territory possesses a monetary allocation;
- no monetary creation is possible.

Any creation of HCN is exclusively conditioned by the activation of a territory through verified human participation.

The territorial activator initiates economic existence within the protocol but obtains no ownership right over the territory or its monetary allocation.

---

### Article 93 — Proof of Humanity

HumChain Network is based on Proof-of-Humanity.

Each natural person may possess only one verified personal account.

The principle:

**"One person. One face. One account."**

guarantees:

- fairness of monetary distribution;
- trust between participants;
- integrity of the protocol.
