# TITLE X — RIGHT OF INVITATION AND TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY

### Article 106 — Object of the Right of Invitation
The right of invitation constitutes a mechanism of human growth intended to promote progressive development of HumChain Network communities.
Its objective is to build a network based on trust, proximity, and responsibility, rather than purely quantitative growth.
The right of invitation constitutes neither a property right over invited persons, nor a mechanism of monetary creation.

### Article 107 — Principle of the Circle of Trust
HumChain Network is based on the principle of the circle of trust.
Each user is invited to develop a community composed of people he or she really knows and with whom he or she has a relationship of trust.
This principle strengthens the quality of proof of humanity, facilitates integration of new members, and promotes cooperation within territories.
Quality of human relationships takes precedence over quantity of invitations.

### Article 108 — Limitation of Invitations
In order to preserve integrity of the network, each user has a maximum right of five (5) direct invitations.
This limitation promotes progressive, controlled, and sustainable growth of HumChain Network.
Modalities of attribution or evolution of this right may be specified by the protocol, without calling into question the principle of development based on trust.

### Article 109 — Territorial Integrity
Any invitation must respect the territorial organization of HumChain Network.
Registration, identification, and activation mechanisms ensure preservation of coherence between territory declared by new user and territorial rules of the protocol.
No invitation may be used to bypass proof of humanity mechanisms or compromise integrity of territories.

### Article 110 — Responsibility of the Sponsor
The sponsor accompanies the new user in his or her integration within HumChain Network.
He or she informs him or her about fundamental principles of the protocol, operating rules, security best practices, and mechanisms for participation in the ecosystem.
This responsibility is moral and communal. It creates no link of property, subordination, or economic dependence between sponsor and invited person.

### Article 111 — Activation of Territorial Communities
The right of invitation contributes to development of local communities.
Each new user strengthens his or her territory by participating in mining, economic exchanges, creation of activities, and dissemination of the mission of HumChain Network.
Development of territories is based on commitment of their inhabitants and their capacity to build a strong local economy before opening to the global economy.

### Article 112 — Protection Against Abuse
Any use of the right of invitation for fraudulent purposes is prohibited.
The following in particular constitute violations of this Constitution:
- creation of fake accounts;
- identity theft;
- circumvention of invitation limit;
- manipulation of proof of humanity mechanisms;
- any organization aiming to divert the right of invitation from its purpose.
Fraudulent behavior may result in sanctions provided for by governance rules.

### Article 113 — Purpose of the Right of Invitation
The right of invitation reflects the vision of HumChain Network that a sustainable economy is built first through authentic human relationships.
Each invitation represents an opportunity to strengthen a community, activate a territory, and develop a local economy.
Thus, the right of invitation directly participates in the mission of the protocol:
Build a global economic infrastructure by strengthening local economies, one verified human at a time.

### Article 114 — Final Provisions
This Constitution enters into force upon its adoption by HumChain Network.
It constitutes the supreme norm of the protocol and is binding on all participants, governance bodies, validators, developers, territorial representatives, merchants, and any person or entity participating in the ecosystem.
Any contrary provision is deemed null and void.
This Constitution is established to ensure that HumChain Network remains faithful to its mission, its founding principles, and its vision, today and for future generations.
