# TITLE X — RIGHT OF INVITATION AND TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY

### Article 102 — Purpose of the Right of Invitation

The right of invitation constitutes a mechanism for human expansion of HumChain Network.

Its purpose is to develop communities based on:

- trust;
- proximity;
- responsibility;
- authentic human relationships.

The right of invitation is neither a property right over invited participants nor a mechanism of monetary creation.

---

### Article 103 — Principle of the Circle of Trust

HumChain Network is based on the principle of the circle of trust.

Each user is encouraged to invite people with whom they have an authentic relationship and whom they can support in their integration into the ecosystem.

This principle strengthens:

- Proof-of-Humanity;
- community quality;
- territorial cooperation.

Quality of human relationships takes precedence over quantity of invitations.

---

### Article 104 — Limitation of Invitations

To preserve network integrity, the protocol establishes limitations on direct invitations.

These limitations aim to:

- prevent artificial growth;
- preserve trust relationships;
- reduce fraudulent behavior.

The exact parameters of invitation mechanisms may evolve according to protocol rules without changing the fundamental principle of trust-based development.
