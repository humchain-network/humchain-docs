# TITLE VIII — PROTOCOL GOVERNANCE

### Article 84 — Principle of Governance

The governance of HumChain Network ensures the evolution of the protocol while preserving its founding mission.

It maintains a balance between:

- innovation;
- stability;
- transparency;
- decentralization;
- protection of constitutional principles.

No governance decision may contradict this Constitution.

---

### Article 85 — Mission of Governance

Governance has the mission to:

- preserve the identity of HumChain Network;
- support responsible protocol evolution;
- guarantee transparency of decisions;
- enable community participation;
- protect the interests of activated territories;
- promote sustainable ecosystem development.

Every governance decision must serve the collective interest of the protocol.

---

### Article 86 — Actors of Governance

Governance is exercised in a decentralized manner by recognized participants of HumChain Network, including:

- verified users;
- merchants;
- validators;
- developers;
- territorial representatives;
- activated territories.

Participation rights are determined by governance rules according to responsibilities and roles within the ecosystem.

Ownership or quantity of HCN alone does not constitute governance authority.

---

### Article 87 — Transparency of Decisions

Any governance proposal must be publicly accessible.

It must specify:

- its objective;
- technical implications;
- economic consequences;
- impact on participants and territories.

Proposals, discussions, decisions, and voting results must be preserved to ensure transparency and traceability.

---

### Article 88 — Evolution of the Protocol

HumChain Network is designed to evolve according to technological, economic, and social developments.

Governance may propose improvements concerning:

- technical infrastructure;
- ecosystem services;
- economic mechanisms;
- operational processes.

Any evolution must strengthen the mission of HumChain Network and respect protected constitutional principles.

---

### Article 89 — Protection of Fundamental Principles

The following principles constitute the permanent foundation of HumChain Network:

- No Human, No Money. Genesis = 0.
- monetary creation conditioned by verified human territorial activation;
- initial global supply equal to 0 HCN;
- maximum allocation of 473,933,649 HCN per activated territory;
- primacy of the real economy;
- human decentralization;
- commercial neutrality of the protocol.

In particular, HumChain Network is not a centralized exchange, decentralized exchange, or trading platform.

It remains a decentralized economic infrastructure supporting real economic activity.

These principles may only be modified through the constitutional revision procedure.

---

### Article 90 — Normative Hierarchy

The fundamental documents of HumChain Network follow the following hierarchy:

1. Constitution;
2. White Paper;
3. Yellow Paper;
4. Governance decisions compatible with the preceding documents.

The Constitution defines the fundamental principles.

The White Paper explains the vision, economic model, and objectives.

The Yellow Paper describes technical foundations and mechanisms.

No lower-ranking document or decision may contradict a higher-ranking document.
