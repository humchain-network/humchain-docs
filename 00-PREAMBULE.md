# PREAMBLE

The global economy rests above all on the women and men who produce, undertake, trade, innovate and cooperate. Since the beginning, wealth has been born from human activity; currency is only its instrument of exchange.

The rise of decentralized technologies has opened a new era by enabling the transfer of value without intermediaries. Yet, much of this innovation has remained centered on digital assets themselves, while the real economy — that of citizens, merchants, artisans, producers, entrepreneurs and territories — remains insufficiently integrated into these infrastructures.

HumChain Network was born from the conviction that technology must serve human beings before serving finance. Its ambition is not to create another blockchain, but to build a global economic infrastructure capable of strengthening local economies and progressively connecting territories to a decentralized network founded on trust, cooperation and real economic activity.

The founding principle of HumChain Network is simple:

**"No Human, No Money. Genesis = 0."**

At protocol creation, no HCN exists. Currency is only created when a first verified human activates a territory. Each activation marks the birth of a new territorial economy and triggers the creation of its monetary allocation, in accordance with the rules defined by the Protocol Constitution.

Thus, monetary creation is no longer arbitrary: it becomes the direct consequence of real human presence. Each activated territory progressively joins a global economy built from authentic local communities.

HumChain Network is based on a clear economic logic:

- the human activates the territory;
- the protocol creates the economic infrastructure;
- mining progressively distributes the currency;
- commerce and entrepreneurship create value;
- national treasuries strengthen the security and development of territories;
- validators secure the network;
- governance sustainably protects the founding vision.

This organization makes HumChain Network an infrastructure where technology is never an end in itself, but a means to connect people, businesses and territories within an open, decentralized and sustainable economy.

This White Paper sets out the vision, economic principles, general functioning and objectives of HumChain Network. It constitutes the founding document of the protocol. The Constitution defines its fundamental rules and institutional guarantees. The Yellow Paper will specify the technical architecture, cryptographic mechanisms and implementation specifications.

HumChain Network carries a simple but demanding ambition: to build the global economy by strengthening local economies, one verified human at a time.
