# Chapter 2 — Layer 1: Human and Monetary Foundation

## 2.1 The Foundation of the Ecosystem

The first layer of HumChain Network establishes the foundation upon which the entire ecosystem is built.

Before commerce, applications or global economic interactions can exist, the network must first establish two essential elements:

- a verified human community;
- a native monetary foundation.

For this reason, Layer 1 combines Proof-of-Humanity with the creation and distribution of HCN.

Together, they provide the conditions necessary for the emergence of a decentralized economy.

---

### 2.2 Proof-of-Humanity

HumChain Network recognizes verified humans—not wallets or anonymous accounts—as the primary participants in the network.

Each participant completes the Proof-of-Humanity process to obtain a unique identity within the protocol.

This mechanism aims to:

- ensure that each participant represents a real human being;
- reduce duplicate or fraudulent identities;
- strengthen trust within the ecosystem;
- establish a fair basis for economic participation.

Human verification is therefore the entry point to the network.

---

### 2.3 Territorial Activation

HumChain Network grows progressively through territorial activation.

A territory becomes active only when its first verified resident joins the network.

Before activation:

- no HCN is allocated to that territory;
- no community mining takes place;
- the territory does not yet participate in the economic ecosystem.

This mechanism reflects the principle that economic development begins with human participation rather than predefined monetary issuance.

---

### 2.4 Genesis = 0 HCN

One of the protocol's fundamental principles is:

**"Genesis = 0 HCN."**

At the global level, no currency exists before territories begin to activate.

As new territories become active, the global supply expands according to the protocol's monetary rules.

This model links monetary creation directly to the progressive inclusion of human communities.

---

### 2.5 Community Mining

After territorial activation, verified participants contribute to the distribution of HCN through Community Mining.

Community Mining is designed to distribute the native currency progressively among verified humans according to the protocol's economic rules.

Its objective is not merely to issue currency, but to establish a broad and inclusive monetary base capable of supporting future economic activity.

---

### 2.6 Building the Monetary Foundation

Layer 1 does not attempt to create an economy immediately.

Its purpose is to establish the essential conditions that make economic development possible:

- verified human participation;
- territorial inclusion;
- progressive monetary distribution;
- a shared economic foundation.

Only after these conditions are in place can the ecosystem naturally evolve toward commerce and economic applications.

---

### 2.7 Conclusion

Layer 1 provides the human and monetary foundation of HumChain Network.

By combining Proof-of-Humanity, territorial activation and progressive monetary creation, it establishes the conditions required for a decentralized economy built on real human participation.

The next layer explains how this monetary foundation becomes an active economy through commerce and decentralized applications.
