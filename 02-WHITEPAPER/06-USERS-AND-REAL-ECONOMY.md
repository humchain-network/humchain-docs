### 6.1 Human at the Heart of the Protocol

HumChain Network is based on a simple conviction: an economy exists only because women and men create value.

Technology organizes exchanges.
Currency facilitates transactions.

But it is people who produce, cultivate, manufacture, teach, transport, heal, trade, innovate, and undertake who truly create wealth.

The protocol was therefore designed around humans rather than around money.

Each user represents a potential economic actor capable of participating in the development of his territory and, progressively, in that of the global economy.

### 6.2 A Unique Digital Identity

To guarantee a fair distribution of currency and preserve trust in the network, HumChain Network applies a fundamental principle:

One Person. One Face. One Account.

Each user has a unique digital identity, created during registration.

This identity is based on several verification elements provided by the protocol:

- declared identity;
- verified phone number;
- facial biometric verification;
- personal security code;
- territory of attachment.

These mechanisms protect the proof-of-humanity and prevent the creation of multiple accounts.

### 6.3 Two Account Categories

HumChain Network distinguishes two main categories of accounts to meet different uses of the ecosystem.

**The Classic Account**

The classic account is intended for individuals.

It allows in particular to participate in mining, to receive, store and transfer HCN, to make payments, to use services offered by HumPay, and to access various applications developed on the protocol.

It constitutes the gateway to the HumChain Network economy.

**The Merchant Account**

The merchant account includes all functionalities of the classic account and also allows exercising an economic activity.

It notably authorizes the creation of one or more digital shops, the sale of goods and services, the use of professional network tools, access to HumAds advertising services, and to future economic services developed for businesses.

Thus, each user can evolve his role within the ecosystem as his activity develops.

### 6.4 Merchants: Engine of Value Creation

HCN has real utility only when it circulates among economic actors.

Merchants, artisans, producers, service providers, entrepreneurs, and businesses therefore occupy a central place in the functioning of HumChain Network.

They transform distributed currency into used currency.

Each sale made, each service rendered, each payment made contributes to developing the local economy and strengthening the utility of HCN.

The protocol thus recognizes commerce as one of the main engines of value creation.

### 6.5 An Economy Built Locally

Economic development follows a natural progression.

Each activity is first born in its territory of origin.

Merchants develop their local clientele, build their reputation, and strengthen their economy before progressively extending their exchanges to other activated territories.

This approach makes it possible to build a solid global economy from truly active local economies.

International growth thus rests on sustainable local foundations.

### 6.6 Services Designed for the Real Economy

HumChain Network is not limited to offering a digital currency.

The protocol progressively provides a complete infrastructure intended to accompany various economic actors.

Among the main services are notably:

- HumWallet, which allows storing and using HCN;
- HumPay, intended for payments between individuals and professionals;
- HumMarket, allowing creation of shops and trade of goods and services;
- HumAds, designed to promote economic activities;
- HumLogistics, intended to facilitate physical exchanges between merchants and consumers.

Together, these services transform the blockchain into a true economic infrastructure.

### 6.7 An Economy Based on Trust

The development of a sustainable digital economy requires a high level of trust.

This trust is based on several principles:

- authenticity of users;
- transparency of rules;
- security of transactions;
- responsibility of merchants;
- integrity of data;
- respect for the Constitution of the protocol.

Each participant thus contributes to the proper functioning of the ecosystem.

Trust is not an additional service.

It constitutes the invisible infrastructure that allows exchanges to develop.

### 6.8 Shared Responsibility

HumChain Network does not rely on a single organization.

Each user becomes responsible for the quality of the network.

Merchants create economic activities.
Users circulate currency.
Developers build tools.
Validators secure infrastructure.
National Treasuries support territorial resilience.
Governance ensures respect for founding principles.

This complementarity allows the protocol to function as a living economy rather than as a simple blockchain.

### 6.9 An Economy Growing with Its Users

Each new user strengthens his territory.
Each new merchant increases the utility of HCN.
Each new shop develops exchanges.
Each new service enriches the ecosystem.

Thus, the growth of HumChain Network does not rely solely on increasing the number of users.

It relies above all on their ability to produce, undertake, collaborate, and create value.

The success of the protocol will not be measured by the quantity of currency distributed, but by the vitality of local economies it will help to emerge.

> Because in HumChain Network, technology serves users, users develop the economy, and the economy progressively builds a more open, more cooperative, and more prosperous world.
