### 5.1 A New Conception of Mining

In most blockchains, mining is associated with the creation of new monetary units. Miners secure the network and receive in return a continuous issuance of new tokens.

HumChain Network adopts a different approach.

Mining does not create currency. Currency already exists upon territory activation. The role of mining is exclusively to progressively distribute the portion of this allocation intended for the community.

Thus, monetary creation and monetary distribution become two distinct mechanisms.

Human creates the supply.
Mining distributes the currency.

This distinction constitutes one of the economic foundations of HumChain Network.

### 5.2 Community Distribution

When a territory is activated, 323,933,649 HCN are reserved for community mining.

This reserve belongs to the economy of the territory. It is not distributed immediately.

The protocol releases it progressively for the benefit of verified inhabitants who participate in the network.

Each user thus contributes to a fair distribution of currency while participating in the development of his community.

Mining becomes a mechanism of economic inclusion rather than a technological competition.

### 5.3 Accessible Participation for All

HumChain Network was designed to allow the greatest number to participate.

Mining does not require specialized hardware or significant investment.

Anyone with a verified account can participate in accordance with protocol rules.

Each participant can own only one personal account, associated with a unique identity, a verified phone number, and facial biometric verification.

The principle is simple:

One Person. One Face. One Account.

This rule guarantees a more equitable distribution of currency and protects the protocol against multiple accounts.

### 5.4 Participation Cycles

Mining operates in successive cycles of six hours.

At the end of each cycle, the user can start a new cycle to continue his participation.

Failure to renew does not cause any penalty.

It simply suspends the distribution of HCN until mining is resumed.

The protocol thus favors voluntary participation rather than permanent obligation.

### 5.5 Progressively Slowing Emission

To preserve the scarcity of HCN, the mining reward progressively decreases.

The initial reward is set at 0.5 HCN per hour, i.e., 3 HCN for a full six-hour cycle.

Each time 500,000 HCN have been distributed in a territory, this reward is reduced by 10%.

This mechanism continues until the complete distribution of the 323,933,649 HCN reserved for mining.

Distribution thus becomes slower and slower over time, favoring early builders while preserving the economic balance of the territory.

### 5.6 The Role of the Security Balance

The first 5 HCN obtained by each miner constitute a security balance.

This amount remains locked as long as the account remains active.

It cannot be transferred, sold, or used.

This guarantee helps limit accounts created solely for speculative or fraudulent purposes and encourages sustainable participation in the network.

### 5.7 Referral for Adoption

HumChain Network recognizes that the growth of an economy relies above all on human relationships.

Each user can invite new verified humans to join the protocol, in compliance with territorial rules.

Referral aims to foster local adoption and strengthen communities.

It is neither an unlimited source of income nor a pyramid system.

The value created by the network comes primarily from the economic activity of territories, not from recruiting new participants.

In HumChain Network, we invite people to build a local economy, not to create a chain of dependency.

### 5.8 The Natural End of Mining

Mining is not permanent.

When a territory has distributed all 323,933,649 HCN reserved for it, mining definitively ends in that territory.

No new currency is created to replace it.

The local economy then continues its development thanks to the circulation of existing HCN, merchants, services built on the protocol, National Treasuries, and exchanges between economic actors.

Mining thus fulfills its mission: ensuring a fair initial distribution before giving way to an autonomous economy.

### 5.9 The Four Economic Pillars of HumChain Network

The economic model of HumChain Network rests on four complementary mechanisms that mutually reinforce each other.

Human creates the supply by activating his territory.
Mining distributes currency to the community.
Referral promotes adoption of new verified humans.
Economic activity sustainably creates value by circulating HCN in daily exchanges.

These four pillars are inseparable.

Removing one of them would weaken the balance of the protocol.

### 5.10 Mining as a Starting Point

Mining is not the finality of HumChain Network.

It represents the first stage of the economic life of a territory.

Once currency is distributed, it is merchants, entrepreneurs, producers, developers, users, and local institutions that give HCN its true utility.

Wealth is not born from mining.

It is born from work, production, exchanges, innovation, and cooperation.

> Mining distributes currency.
> The real economy gives it value.
