### 5.1 A New Conception of Mining

In many blockchain systems, mining is directly associated with the creation of new monetary units. Participants secure the network and receive newly issued tokens as a reward.

HumChain Network adopts a different economic model.

Mining does not create new HCN.

The monetary allocation already exists when a territory is activated by a verified human. The role of mining is to progressively distribute the portion of this allocation reserved for the community.

Therefore, HumChain Network separates two fundamental processes:

**Human presence creates the monetary supply.**
**Mining distributes the currency to the community.**

This distinction represents one of the core principles of the protocol.

---

### 5.2 Community Distribution

When a territory is activated, 323,933,649 HCN are reserved for community mining.

This allocation belongs to the economic foundation of the territory.

It is not released immediately.

The protocol progressively distributes these HCN among verified inhabitants participating in the network according to predefined rules.

Mining therefore serves as a mechanism of economic inclusion, allowing community members to progressively receive a share of the territorial allocation while contributing to the development of their local economy.

---

### 5.3 Accessible Participation for All

HumChain Network was designed to enable broad participation.

Mining does not require specialized hardware, large financial resources, or technical expertise.

Any verified human can participate according to protocol rules.

Each participant is associated with a unique verified identity, ensuring that each individual can maintain only one account.

The principle is:

**One Person. One Face. One Account.**

This mechanism protects the network against artificial account creation and ensures a more balanced distribution of currency among real human participants.
