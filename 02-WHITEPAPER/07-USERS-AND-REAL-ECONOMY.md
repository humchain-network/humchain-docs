# CHAPTER 6 — USERS AND THE REAL ECONOMY

### 6.1 Human Beings at the Heart of the Protocol

HumChain Network is founded on a simple conviction:

An economy exists because human beings create value.

Technology provides infrastructure.

Currency facilitates exchanges.

But it is people who produce, cultivate, manufacture, teach, transport, provide services, trade, innovate, and undertake who create real economic value.

For this reason, HumChain Network is designed around human participation rather than around monetary speculation.

Each verified user represents a potential economic actor capable of contributing to the development of his territory and progressively participating in a connected global economy.

---

### 6.2 A Unique Digital Identity

A sustainable economic network requires trust between participants.

To protect fair participation and preserve the integrity of monetary distribution, HumChain Network applies a fundamental principle:

**One Person. One Face. One Account.**

Each user possesses a unique digital identity created during registration.

This identity relies on protocol-defined verification mechanisms, including:

- declared identity information;
- verified phone number;
- facial biometric verification;
- personal security credentials;
- territorial attachment.

These mechanisms support the Proof-of-Humanity principle and help prevent artificial account creation.

The objective is simple:

A monetary system built for humans must first be able to recognize real human participation.

---

### 6.3 User Participation Within the Ecosystem

HumChain Network allows users to participate according to their role and economic activity.

Every verified user can become an active participant in the ecosystem by:

- participating in community mining;
- holding and managing HCN through personal wallets;
- using ecosystem services;
- participating in economic exchanges;
- contributing to the development of local communities.

The user is not only a beneficiary of the network.

He is one of the actors who makes the network economically alive.

---

### 6.4 Individual Accounts and Merchant Accounts

HumChain Network distinguishes between personal participation and professional economic activity.

**The Individual Account**

The individual account is designed for personal users.

It provides access to essential ecosystem functions, including:

- receiving, storing, and transferring HCN;
- participating in community mining;
- making payments within supported ecosystem services;
- accessing applications developed on HumChain Network.

It represents the entry point into the decentralized economic infrastructure.
