# Economic Constitution - HumChain Network

### One identical allocation per territory

To ensure equality between territories, each activation gives rise to a fixed allocation of:
**473,933,649 HCN**

This allocation is distributed across four complementary components:

- **323,933,649 HCN** for community mining (Proof-of-Humanity);
- **25,000,000 HCN** for the National Treasury;
- **50,000,000 HCN** for public sale and ecosystem access;
- **75,000,000 HCN** for protocol development, validators, and merchant financing.

This distribution is defined by the Constitution of HumChain Network and constitutes the fundamental economic equilibrium of the protocol.

**No territory may receive a higher allocation.**
