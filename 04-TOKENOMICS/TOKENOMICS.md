# Economic Constitution - HumChain Network

### The Fundamental Equilibrium
HumChain has no pre-mine. Supply is created only by human activation.
Genesis = 0.

### One Identical Allocation Per Territory
To ensure absolute equality between nations, each activation mints a fixed allocation of:

**473,933,649 HCN**

This allocation is identical for every territory, regardless of size, population, or GDP.

### Distribution - The Four Pillars
This allocation is distributed across four complementary and immutable components:

| Pillar | Allocation | % | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Community Mining** | 323,933,649 HCN | 68.35% | Proof-of-Humanity mining by verified humans |
| **2. National Treasury** | 25,000,000 HCN | 5.27% | Sovereign fund managed by the territory |
| **3. Public Sale** | 50,000,000 HCN | 10.55% | Ecosystem Access & Growth |
| **4. Protocol & Validators** | 75,000,000 HCN | 15.83% | Infrastructure & Merchant Financing |
| **TOTAL** | **473,933,649 HCN** | **100%** |  |

### The Law
`Total Supply = N_activated * 473,933,649 HCN`

- `N_activated` = number of territories activated by a Genesis Human.
- Reference: 249 territories (ISO 3166-1).
- Max theoretical supply if all activate: 118,009,478,601 HCN.

> **No territory may receive a higher allocation. No territory may receive less.**
> This distribution is the fundamental economic equilibrium of HumChain and cannot be altered.
