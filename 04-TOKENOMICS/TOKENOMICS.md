# Economic Constitution - HumChain Network

### The Fundamental Equilibrium

HumChain has no pre-mine. Supply is created only by human activation.

### One Identical Allocation Per Territory

To ensure equality between territories, each activation mints a fixed allocation of:

**473,933,649 HCN**

This allocation is identical for every territory, regardless of size, population, or GDP.

### Distribution - The Four Pillars

This allocation is distributed across four complementary and immutable components, as defined by the Constitution:

- **323,933,649 HCN (68.35%)** - Community Mining (Proof-of-Humanity)
  Mining by verified humans of the territory.

- **25,000,000 HCN (5.27%)** - National Treasury
  Sovereign fund managed by the territory.

- **50,000,000 HCN (10.55%)** - Public Sale and Ecosystem Access
  Open access for ecosystem growth.

- **75,000,000 HCN (15.83%)** - Protocol Development, Validators, and Merchant Financing
  Infrastructure and adoption.

**Total per territory: 473,933,649 HCN**

### The Law

`Total Supply = N_activated * 473,933,649 HCN`

- N_activated = number of territories activated by a Genesis Human.
- Reference today: 249 territories per ISO 3166-1.
- Max theoretical supply if all activate: 118,009,478,601 HCN.

This number is not locked in the protocol. The protocol only knows N_activated.

**No territory may receive a higher allocation. No territory may receive less.**

This distribution constitutes the fundamental economic equilibrium of HumChain and cannot be altered.
