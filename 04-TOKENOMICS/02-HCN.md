# Chapter 2 — The HCN

## 2.1 The Native Currency of HumChain Network

HCN (HumChain Network Coin) is the native currency of HumChain Network.

It is the official economic unit of the protocol and serves as the common medium of exchange between participants of the ecosystem, including users, merchants, businesses, developers, validators, and communities of activated territories.

HCN is not designed as a simple digital asset created primarily for holding or speculation.

It is designed as the native currency of a decentralized economic infrastructure whose purpose is to enable value exchange generated by human activity.

Its existence directly serves the mission of HumChain Network:

**To build a global economic infrastructure by strengthening local economies.**

HumChain Network does not operate as a centralized exchange, decentralized exchange, or trading platform.

External services may provide exchange capabilities, but trading is not a native function of the protocol.

### 2.2 A Currency Born from Humans

Unlike models where monetary supply is created before human participation exists, HCN follows the opposite logic.

At protocol genesis:

- global supply is 0 HCN;
- no territory holds monetary allocation;
- no reserve exists;
- no actor receives a pre-allocation.

Monetary creation begins only when a territory is activated by its first verified inhabitant.

Each unit of HCN therefore originates from a real human presence.

This approach ensures that currency follows community development instead of preceding it.

> **Technical note:** Activation creates the constitutional allocation of **473,933,649 HCN** assigned to the activated territory. No tokens are pre-mined. Distribution occurs progressively through Proof-of-Humanity Mining and constitutional mechanisms.

### 2.3 Economic Functions of HCN

HCN fulfills several complementary functions within the protocol and its ecosystem.

It enables:

- peer-to-peer payments;
- merchant settlements;
- protocol transaction fees;
- use of applications built on HumChain infrastructure;
- development of merchant activities;
- visibility services such as HumAds;
- payment services such as HumPay;
- participation in economic mechanisms defined by the protocol.

HCN is therefore a utility currency before being considered a financial asset.

### 2.4 A Currency for the Real Economy

The economic role of HCN comes primarily from its utility.

As users, merchants, businesses, and territories participate in economic activities, HCN becomes a medium facilitating exchanges and coordination.

The protocol does not artificially create economic demand.

It provides decentralized infrastructure where real economic activity can generate practical utility.

HCN is designed to support commerce, not replace economic actors.

### 2.5 A Territorial and Global Currency

Each activated territory receives an identical constitutional allocation.

This principle ensures equal economic foundations between territories.

However, HCN is not restricted to territorial boundaries.

As territories become activated and interconnected, HCN enables exchanges:

- between individuals within communities;
- between businesses from different territories;
- between continents;
- across the global HumChain economy.

HCN creates economic connectivity while preserving territorial autonomy.

### 2.6 Divisibility

HCN is divisible up to 16 decimals.

**1 HCN = 10¹⁶ phii** (smallest unit).

This precision enables:

- micro-transactions;
- low-value payments;
- digital services;
- automated protocol operations;
- international economic exchanges.

### 2.7 Core Principles of HCN

The economic operation of HCN is based on permanent principles:

- global initial supply fixed at 0 HCN;
- monetary creation exclusively linked to human activation of territories;
- identical maximum allocation for each activated territory (473,933,649 HCN);
- progressive distribution through mining;
- permanent destruction mechanisms defined by the protocol;
- utility grounded in the real economy;
- governance protecting economic stability;
- predictable global maximum supply:

**473,933,649 HCN × number of activated territories**

These principles guarantee the coherence, predictability, and sustainability of HCN.

### 2.8 A Currency for Trust

HCN is not only a unit of account.

It represents a vision where trust becomes a fundamental foundation of economic coordination.

By linking monetary creation to proof-of-humanity, encouraging local economic development before global interconnection, and placing human activity at the center of its model, HumChain Network defines HCN as a currency designed for a sustainable economy.

HCN is therefore not simply a digital asset.

It is the native economic instrument of a decentralized infrastructure where technology remains in service of people, communities, and territories.
