# Chapter 2 — The HCN

## 2.1 The Native Currency of HumChain Network

The HCN (HumChain Network Coin) is the native currency of HumChain Network.

It is the fundamental economic unit of the protocol and serves as a common medium of exchange between users, merchants, businesses, developers, validators, and activated territories.

HCN is not a simple digital asset designed primarily for holding or speculation.

It is designed as the currency of a decentralized economic infrastructure that enables independent economic actors to exchange value, coordinate activities, and participate in an open digital economy.

Its existence directly serves the mission of HumChain Network:

**To build a global economic infrastructure by strengthening local economies.**

HumChain Network provides the infrastructure.

Human activities create the economy.

---

### 2.2 A Currency Born from Humans

Unlike models in which monetary supply is created before communities exist, HCN follows the opposite logic.

At protocol genesis:

- global supply is 0 HCN;
- no territory holds any currency;
- no reserve is created;
- no actor receives a pre-allocation.

HumChain Network provides for neither pre-mining nor initial global issuance.

Monetary creation begins only when a territory is activated by its first verified inhabitant.

Each unit of HCN therefore originates from a real human presence.

This approach ensures that currency follows community development instead of preceding it.

> **Technical note:** Activation unlocks a constitutional allocation of **473,933,649 HCN** for the territory contract. No tokens are pre-mined. Release occurs exclusively through protocol-defined mechanisms, including Proof-of-Humanity Mining.

---

### 2.3 Economic Functions of HCN

HCN fulfills several complementary functions within the decentralized infrastructure of HumChain Network.

It enables:

- peer-to-peer payments;
- merchant and business settlements;
- network fees;
- access to protocol services;
- economic applications built on or connected to HumChain Network;
- creation and development of independent digital storefronts;
- visibility services such as HumAds;
- participation in the economic mechanisms defined by the protocol.

HCN does not replace economic actors.

It provides a common monetary infrastructure allowing them to interact.

---

### 2.4 A Currency for the Real Economy

The value of HCN rests primarily on its economic utility.

The more users, merchants, businesses, and territories use HCN to exchange value, the stronger its role as a medium of economic coordination becomes.

HumChain Network does not create commercial value by itself and does not determine prices, products, or business activities.

Economic value is created by humans through:

- production;
- commerce;
- services;
- entrepreneurship;
- innovation;
- cooperation.

The protocol provides the infrastructure that allows this value to circulate.

HCN is therefore designed to support commerce, not to replace it.

---

### 2.5 A Territorial and Global Currency

Each activated territory receives an identical constitutional allocation.

This allocation allows each local economy to begin from a common foundation.

However, HCN usage is not limited by territorial borders.

As new territories are activated, their economies become progressively interconnected.

HCN facilitates exchanges:

- between citizens of the same territory;
- between independent businesses from different territories;
- between communities across continents;
- within the global economic infrastructure of HumChain Network.

HumChain Network does not control these economic relationships.

It provides the decentralized infrastructure that allows them to develop.

---

### 2.6 Divisibility

HCN is divisible up to 16 decimals.

**1 HCN = 10^16 phii** (smallest unit).

This precision enables:

- micro-transactions;
- low-value payments;
- digital services;
- automated transactions;
- international exchanges.

Divisibility ensures that HCN can support economic activity at different scales.

---

### 2.7 Core Principles of HCN

The economic operation of HCN is based on permanent principles:

- global initial supply fixed at 0 HCN;
- monetary creation exclusively linked to human activation of territories;
- identical maximum allocation for each activated territory (473,933,649 HCN);
- progressive distribution of currency through mining;
- permanent destruction of a portion of circulating currency;
- utility grounded in real economic activity;
- neutrality of the protocol regarding commercial activities;
- governance mechanisms ensuring stability of the economic model;
- predictable supply based on activated territories.

These principles guarantee the coherence, transparency, and sustainability of HCN.

---

### 2.8 A Currency for Trust

HCN is not intended to be solely a unit of account or a financial instrument.

It represents a vision in which trust becomes a fundamental infrastructure of economic coordination.

By linking monetary creation to proof-of-humanity, favoring local economic development before global exchanges, and placing human activity at the center of its operation, HumChain Network creates a currency designed to support sustainable economic interaction.

HCN is therefore more than a digital asset.

It is the economic foundation of a decentralized infrastructure where technology remains at the service of people, communities, territories, and independent economic actors.

**HumChain Network provides the infrastructure.**

**Humans create the economy.**
