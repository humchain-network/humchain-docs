### Chapter 2 — The HCN

#### 2.1 The Native Currency of HumChain Network

The HCN (HumChain Network Coin) is the native currency of HumChain Network.

It is the official economic unit of the entire protocol and represents the common medium of exchange between users, merchants, businesses, developers, validators, and activated territories.

HCN is not a simple digital token designed to be held or traded on markets.

It is designed as the currency of a global economic infrastructure whose objective is to facilitate value exchanges derived from human activity.

Its existence directly serves the mission of HumChain Network:

**To build a global economic infrastructure by strengthening local economies.**

#### 2.2 A Currency Born from Humans

Unlike models in which monetary supply is created before users arrive, HCN follows the opposite logic.

At protocol genesis:

- global supply is 0 HCN;
- no territory holds any currency;
- no reserve is created;
- no actor receives a pre-allocation.

Monetary creation begins only when a territory is activated by its first verified inhabitant.

Each unit of HCN thus originates from a real human presence.

This approach ensures that currency follows community development instead of preceding it.

*Technical note: Activation unlocks a constitutional allocation of 473M HCN in the territory contract. No tokens are pre-mined. Release occurs exclusively through Proof-of-Humanity Mining.*

#### 2.3 Economic Functions of HCN

HCN fulfills several complementary functions within the ecosystem.

It enables:

- peer-to-peer payments;
- merchant settlements;
- network fees (gas);
- use of ecosystem dApps;
- creation and development of digital storefronts;
- funding visibility campaigns via HumAds;
- access to services provided by HumPay;
- participation in the protocol's economic mechanisms.

HCN is therefore a currency of utility before being a financial asset.

#### 2.4 A Currency for the Real Economy

The value of HCN rests primarily on its utility.

The more users, merchants, businesses, and territories use HCN to produce, sell, buy, and exchange, the stronger its economic role becomes.

The protocol does not seek to artificially create demand.

It provides an infrastructure in which economic activity naturally generates currency usage.

HCN is thus designed to support commerce, not replace it.

#### 2.5 A Territorial and Global Currency

Each activated territory receives an identical constitutional allocation.

This allocation allows each local economy to start on a common basis.

However, HCN usage is not limited to territorial borders.

As new territories are activated, their economies become interconnected.

HCN then facilitates exchanges:

- between citizens of the same territory;
- between businesses from different territories;
- between continents;
- within the global economy of HumChain Network.

It thus constitutes an economic link between communities while respecting the autonomy of each territory.

#### 2.6 Divisibility

HCN is divisible up to 16 decimals.

1 HCN = 10^16 phii (smallest unit).

This precision enables:

- micro-transactions;
- low-value payments;
- digital services;
- automated transactions;
- international exchanges.

Divisibility ensures HCN can sustainably support ecosystem growth, regardless of adoption dynamics.

#### 2.7 Core Principles of HCN

The economic operation of HCN is based on permanent principles:

- global initial supply fixed at 0 HCN;
- monetary creation exclusively linked to human activation of territories;
- identical maximum allocation for each activated territory (473M HCN);
- progressive distribution of currency through mining;
- permanent destruction (burn) of a portion of circulating currency;
- utility grounded in the real economy;
- governance ensuring stability of the economic model;
- capped and predictable global supply (473M x number of UN-recognized territories).

These principles guarantee the coherence, predictability, and sustainability of HCN.

#### 2.8 A Currency for Trust

HCN is not intended to be solely a unit of account.

It reflects a vision in which trust is the primary wealth of an economy.

By linking monetary creation to proof-of-humanity, by favoring local exchanges before global ones, and by placing economic activity at the heart of its operation, HumChain Network makes HCN a currency designed to support a sustainable economy.

HCN is thus much more than a digital asset.

It is the economic expression of a protocol where technology remains in service of people, communities, and territories.
