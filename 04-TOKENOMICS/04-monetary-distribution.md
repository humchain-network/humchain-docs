# Chapter 4 — Monetary Distribution

## 4.1 Creating Currency Is Not Distributing It

In HumChain Network, monetary creation and monetary distribution are two distinct mechanisms.

Creation occurs only once, upon activation of a territory by its first verified inhabitant.

Distribution corresponds to the progressive release of this already-created currency into circulation.

This separation constitutes one of the foundations of the economic model of HumChain Network.

Currency exists before it is distributed, but it enters economic circulation according to human participation and protocol-defined mechanisms.

---

### 4.2 Mining as a Distribution Mechanism

Mining does not create new HCN.

It progressively distributes the **323,933,649 HCN** reserved for community mining of each activated territory.

Each verified user participates in the distribution of their territory's allocated currency according to the rules defined by the protocol.

Mining rewards human participation in the network and contributes to the progressive circulation of HCN among verified participants.

It never modifies the maximum supply defined by the Constitution.

---

### 4.3 A Distribution Reserved for Verified Humans

Only users who satisfy the proof-of-humanity requirements may participate in mining.

Each participant must have:

- a unique personal account;
- a verified phone number;
- an identity compliant with protocol rules;
- validated biometric verification.

The principle remains:

**One person. One face. One account.**

This requirement ensures that monetary distribution is linked to real human participation and protects the integrity of the protocol.

---

### 4.4 A Progressive Distribution

HCN allocated to mining are never distributed at once.

They are released progressively according to parameters defined by the protocol.

This progression allows:

- sustainable community growth;
- controlled monetary circulation;
- preservation of economic balance within territories;
- long-term participation of users.

The distribution rate is therefore designed to accompany the progressive development of the decentralized infrastructure.

---

### 4.5 Progressive Reduction of Rewards

To preserve HCN scarcity, mining rewards decrease progressively.

This reduction is programmed by the protocol and occurs as the mining allocation is distributed.

It pursues several objectives:

- extend the duration of monetary distribution;
- encourage long-term participation;
- maintain balance between early and future participants;
- prevent excessively rapid circulation.

The exact reduction mechanism is defined by the technical specifications of the protocol.

---

### 4.6 End of Mining Distribution

Distribution ends when all **323,933,649 HCN** reserved for mining of a territory have been distributed.

From that moment:

- no new mining rewards are created for that territory;
- the maximum supply of the territory remains unchanged;
- HCN continues to circulate between independent economic actors.

The end of mining does not represent the end of the territory's economy.

It represents the transition from protocol-based distribution toward an economy driven primarily by human activity, exchanges, services, and economic cooperation.

---

### 4.7 A Distribution Serving Economic Activity

Mining constitutes the initial mechanism through which HCN enters circulation.

However, its purpose is not to encourage passive accumulation.

The distributed HCN are intended to enable participation in the economic life of the network, including:

- exchanges of goods and services between independent actors;
- development of economic activities;
- access to protocol services;
- participation in local and cross-territory economic interactions.

HumChain Network does not create economic value itself.

It provides the infrastructure that allows human activities to exchange and coordinate value.

---

### 4.8 An Economy Taking Over

As distribution progresses, the relative importance of mining decreases in favor of economic activity.

Commerce, services, entrepreneurship, production, and exchanges between participants progressively become the main drivers of HCN circulation.

Thus:

**Monetary creation initiates the economy.**

**Mining distributes currency.**

**Human activity gives it lasting utility.**

This transition represents the objective of HumChain Network: transforming a currency initially distributed by protocol mechanisms into a currency supported by a real, dynamic, and autonomous economy.

**HumChain Network provides the infrastructure.**
**Humans create the economy.**
