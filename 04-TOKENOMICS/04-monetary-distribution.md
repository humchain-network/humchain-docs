### Chapter 4 — Monetary Distribution

#### 4.1 Creating Currency Is Not Distributing It

In HumChain Network, monetary creation and monetary distribution are two distinct mechanisms.

Creation occurs only once, upon activation of a territory by its first verified inhabitant.

Distribution corresponds to the progressive release of this currency into circulation.

This separation constitutes one of the foundations of the economic model of HumChain Network.

Currency exists before it is distributed, but it only enters the economy at the pace of human participation and network activity.

#### 4.2 Mining as a Distribution Mechanism

Mining does not create new HCN.

It progressively distributes the 323,933,649 HCN reserved for community mining of each activated territory.

Each verified user thus participates in an equitable distribution of their territory's currency.

Mining rewards participation in the protocol and contributes to a progressive diffusion of HCN within the community.

It never modifies the maximum supply defined by the Constitution.

#### 4.3 A Distribution Reserved for Verified Humans

Only users who have satisfied the proof-of-humanity requirements can participate in mining.

Each participant must have:

- a unique personal account;
- a verified phone number;
- an identity compliant with protocol rules;
- a validated biometric verification.

The principle remains:

**One person. One face. One account.**

This requirement ensures that monetary distribution benefits real people and limits fraud risks.

#### 4.4 A Progressive Distribution

HCN allocated to mining are never distributed at once.

They are released progressively according to parameters defined by the protocol.

This progression allows:

- to support community development;
- to avoid excessive circulation of currency;
- to preserve the economic balance of the territory;
- to encourage sustainable participation of users.

The distribution speed is thus adapted to the real growth of the ecosystem.

#### 4.5 Progressive Reduction of Rewards

In order to preserve HCN scarcity, mining rewards decrease progressively.

This decrease is programmed by the protocol and occurs as the mining allocation is distributed.

It pursues several objectives:

- extend the distribution duration;
- favor long-term adoption;
- maintain balance between early and future generations of users;
- limit effects of too rapid emission.

The exact reduction mechanism is defined by the technical specifications of the protocol.

#### 4.6 End of Mining Distribution

Distribution ends when all 323,933,649 HCN reserved for mining of a territory have been distributed.

From that moment:

- no new mining rewards are created for that territory;
- the maximum supply of the territory remains unchanged;
- HCN continues to circulate through economic exchanges between actors.

The end of mining therefore does not mark the end of the territory's economy.

It marks the beginning of an economy based exclusively on exchanges, payments, commerce, and services.

#### 4.7 A Distribution Serving the Economy

Mining constitutes the entry point of currency into the economy.

However, its purpose is not to encourage passive accumulation.

The protocol is designed so that distributed HCN are used:

- to buy and sell goods and services;
- to develop economic activities;
- to finance projects;
- to access ecosystem services;
- to strengthen exchanges between territories.

Monetary distribution thus prepares the development of the real economy.

#### 4.8 An Economy Taking Over

As distribution progresses, the role of mining decreases in favor of economic activity.

Commerce, services, entrepreneurship, and exchanges progressively become the main drivers of HCN circulation.

Thus, monetary creation initiates the economy.

Mining diffuses currency.

But it is human activity that sustainably gives it value.

This transition constitutes the objective sought by HumChain Network: to evolve a currency distributed by the protocol into a currency carried by a real, dynamic, and autonomous economy.
