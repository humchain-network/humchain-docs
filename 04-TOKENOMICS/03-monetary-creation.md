### Chapter 3 — Monetary Creation

#### 3.1 An Unprecedented Model of Monetary Creation

The monetary creation of HumChain Network is based on a fundamental principle that distinguishes the protocol from traditional monetary models and most digital assets.

At protocol launch:

- total supply is 0 HCN;
- no territory holds any currency;
- no allocation is created;
- no actor benefits from early issuance.

HumChain Network provides for neither pre-mining nor initial global issuance.

All monetary creation depends exclusively on the arrival of verified human beings within the network.

This is why the protocol states:

**"No human, no money. Genesis = 0."**

#### 3.2 Territory Activation

Each territory recognized by HumChain Network remains inactive as long as no verified inhabitant registers there.

The first verified inhabitant becomes the activator of the territory.

Their registration automatically triggers:

- the official activation of the territory;
- the creation of its constitutional allocation;
- the opening of its national treasury;
- the start of community mining;
- the integration of the territory into the global economy of HumChain Network.

The activator initiates the digital economy of their territory but never becomes its owner.

They exercise a founding role without benefiting from a permanent privilege.

#### 3.3 An Identical Allocation for Each Territory

Each activated territory receives a maximum allocation of:

**473,933,649 HCN**

This allocation is identical for all territories to guarantee economic equality between communities joining HumChain Network.

The protocol does not favor any country based on:

- its population;
- its surface area;
- its level of development;
- its economic power;
- its political influence.

Each territory starts with the same monetary base.

Future differences in prosperity will depend solely on economic activity, HCN adoption, and the ability of communities to create value.

#### 3.4 Allocation Breakdown

The territorial allocation is permanently distributed across four components.

**Community Mining**

**323,933,649 HCN**

This share is progressively distributed to verified inhabitants participating in mining.

It promotes a broad and equitable distribution of currency.

**National Treasury**

**25,000,000 HCN**

This allocation is intended to strengthen the security, resilience, and economic development of the territory.

**Public Sale**

**50,000,000 HCN**

This allocation is intended to support the economic launch of the territory, foster HCN liquidity, and support ecosystem adoption.

**Protocol Development**

**75,000,000 HCN**

This allocation finances:

- technical development;
- validators;
- infrastructures;
- economic tools;
- business development;
- strategic partners of the protocol.

This breakdown is fixed by the Constitution and can only be modified through a constitutional revision.

#### 3.5 An Evolving Global Supply

Unlike models where the maximum supply is known from day one, HumChain Network progressively builds its global supply.

Each territorial activation increases the total supply of the network.

Thus, the final global supply will depend on the number of territories activated in accordance with the protocol rules.

This approach makes the total supply a true global counter of human inclusion.

Each activated territory represents a new community integrated into the global economy of HumChain Network.

#### 3.6 A Unique Monetary Creation

Each territory can only be activated once.

Consequently:

- only one allocation of 473,933,649 HCN can be created for a territory;
- no additional issuance can be granted subsequently;
- no authority can create additional HCN for an already activated territory.

This rule guarantees the predictability and stability of the economic model.

#### 3.7 Monetary Creation Independent of Speculation

Monetary creation is linked to:

- neither the price of HCN;
- nor trading volumes;
- nor financial markets;
- nor speculative demand.

It depends exclusively on the activation of territories by verified human beings.

Thus, monetary issuance accompanies the real growth of the network rather than market fluctuations.

#### 3.8 Economic Consequences

This mechanism produces several structuring effects:

- it eliminates pre-mining and initial privileges;
- it guarantees equality of starting point between territories;
- it directly associates currency with human presence;
- it favors progressive distribution rather than immediate concentration;
- it supports the building of a global economy based on the development of local economies.

Monetary creation thus becomes a reflection of the human expansion of the protocol and not the result of a centralized decision.

It constitutes one of the foundations of the economic stability of HumChain Network.
