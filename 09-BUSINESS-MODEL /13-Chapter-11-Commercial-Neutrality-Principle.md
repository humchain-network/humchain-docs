# Chapter 11: The Principle of Commercial Neutrality

### Introduction

HumChain Network is designed as a decentralized economic infrastructure, not as a crypto-asset trading platform.

Its mission is to organize economic exchanges between individuals, merchants, producers, artisans, businesses, institutions, and the various marketplaces built on the network.

The protocol does not seek to become a player in the financial exchange market. It provides the infrastructure upon which a sustainable digital economy can develop.

### The Separation Between Infrastructure and Market

HumChain Network deliberately distinguishes two complementary but independent functions.

The first is the creation of economic value through commerce, goods, services, and applications developed within the ecosystem.

The second is the exchange of HCN for other digital assets or local currencies.

These two functions address different needs and do not need to be provided by the same infrastructure.

### The Role of HCN

HCN is the native currency of HumChain Network.

It is intended to circulate within the ecosystem to enable payments, access to services, the operation of marketplaces, and all economic activities offered by the protocol.

Its primary purpose is to power a real economy built on the HumChain infrastructure.

### Asset Custody

Users keep their HCN in their own digital wallets.

HumChain Network does not custody its users' assets and does not act as a financial intermediary.

Each user remains fully responsible for holding and using their funds.

### Trading as an External Service

Trading, buying, selling, or converting HCN can be carried out on specialized platforms, whether centralized (CEX) or decentralized (DEX).

These platforms fulfill a function that is complementary to HumChain Network but are not part of its economic infrastructure.

The protocol remains neutral with regard to the platforms used by users.

### Benefits of the Commercial Neutrality Principle

This choice allows HumChain Network to focus its resources on its fundamental mission:

- developing an open economic infrastructure;
- fostering commerce and the exchange of goods and services;
- connecting economic actors to different marketplaces;
- strengthening local economies before their integration into a global economy;
- preserving the neutrality of the protocol with respect to trading activities.

Thus, HumChain Network is not designed to become an exchange. It is designed to become the economic infrastructure on which value exchanges find concrete utility in real economic life.
