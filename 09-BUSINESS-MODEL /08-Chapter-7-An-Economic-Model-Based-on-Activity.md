# Part IV — Business Model
## Chapter 7 — An Economic Model Based on Activity

### 7.1 An open infrastructure in service of the economy

HumChain Network was not designed to sell access to a platform.

Its purpose is to provide an open economic infrastructure on which communities, businesses, merchants, entrepreneurs, and other actors can develop their activities.

The objective is not to generate revenue through user registration.

The objective is to enable the development of an economy where each activity contributes to the dynamism of the entire network.

In this approach, HumChain's growth is directly linked to the growth of economic exchanges it makes possible to organize.

### 7.2 HCN as an economic engine

HCN is the native currency of HumChain Network.

It constitutes the common economic unit used by the different services of the ecosystem.

As new economic activities appear, HCN circulates among participants to support exchanges defined by the protocol and applications developed on the infrastructure.

The objective is not for the currency to exist independently of the economy.

The objective is for its utility to grow with the development of economic activity.

Thus, the more the infrastructure is used, the more opportunities to use HCN multiply.

### 7.3 An economy powered by services

HumChain does not rely on a single application.

The infrastructure allows the development of numerous economic services meeting the needs of different actors in the network.

As an example, the ecosystem can host:

- specialized marketplaces;
- payment solutions;
- advertising services;
- tools for merchants and businesses;
- applications developed by independent operators;
- services adapted to institutions and organizations.

Each new service strengthens the utility of the infrastructure and contributes to the creation of a richer economic ecosystem.

### 7.4 A common economic participation

Economic actors who develop their activities on HumChain participate in an economy based on the same infrastructure.

Economic interactions defined by the protocol use HCN as the native currency.

This approach creates an environment where exchanges, services, and economic activities can operate around a common unit, thus promoting the coherence of the ecosystem.

HCN becomes the economic link between the different participants in the network.

### 7.5 Sustainable growth

HumChain considers that the sustainability of an economic infrastructure does not depend solely on its number of users.

It depends on the ability of this infrastructure to generate useful economic activities.

Each store that opens.

Each marketplace that develops.

Each service that is created.

Each transaction carried out.

Each territory that joins the network.

Contributes to strengthening the utility of the infrastructure for all participants.

This logic allows HumChain to build an economic model where the growth of the ecosystem is driven by real economic activity rather than by artificial value creation.

### 7.6 An economy created by its participants

HumChain Network does not seek to build a global economy alone.

The infrastructure is designed to allow millions of economic actors to develop their own initiatives.

Each merchant.

Each entrepreneur.

Each company.

Each producer.

Each institution.

Participates in the development of the network.

Thus, HumChain does not create the economy.

It provides the infrastructure allowing human beings to build it together.
