# Part VI — Trust, Governance, and Economic Vision
## Chapter 10 — Trust as the Foundation of the Economy

### 10.1 Trust, the primary wealth of an economy

Any economy rests on an invisible but essential resource: trust.

Exchanges can only develop sustainably when participants have confidence in the people they interact with, in the rules that organize the network, and in the infrastructure that facilitates these exchanges.

HumChain Network considers that trust is not a consequence of economic development.

It is its condition.

That is why trust constitutes one of the foundations of its economic architecture.

### 10.2 Proof of Humanity

HumChain is based on a simple principle:

A human economy must be built by human beings.

Proof of Humanity helps to strengthen the integrity of the network by limiting the creation of multiple identities and promoting participation based on real people.

This approach contributes to preserving the equity of the economic model and to strengthening trust between different participants.

The objective is not only to verify an identity.

The objective is to protect an economy built around authentic human communities.

### 10.3 Protection of personal data

HumChain recognizes that trust also requires respect for privacy.

Information necessary for identity verification is processed according to the principles of data minimization and confidentiality protection.

The objective is to confirm the uniqueness of each participant while limiting the retention of personal information to the strict minimum, in accordance with the operating rules of the protocol and applicable legal requirements.

Digital trust does not rest solely on technical security.

It also rests on responsible management of personal data.

### 10.4 Governance based on territories

HumChain adopts a governance that favors territorial representation rather than concentration of economic power.

The development of the network is driven by communities organized through their territories.

This approach aims to ensure that important decisions reflect the economic realities of participating communities rather than only interests linked to holding the native currency.

Governance thus becomes a tool for balance between territories and for preservation of the economic vision of the network.

### 10.5 Shared responsibility

HumChain considers that the success of a global economic infrastructure cannot rest on a single organization.

It depends on the commitment of all participants.

Each user.

Each merchant.

Each entrepreneur.

Each developer.

Each territorial representative.

Each institution.

Contributes, at its level, to the development of the ecosystem.

The economy thus becomes a collective work where everyone participates in value creation.

### 10.6 Vision

HumChain Network aims to build a global economic infrastructure where technology remains in service of human beings.

An infrastructure in which local economies become the foundations of a more balanced, more inclusive, and more resilient global economy.

An infrastructure where visibility promotes opportunities.

Where relationships strengthen trust.

Where exchanges create value.

And where each territory contributes to the prosperity of the entire network.

HumChain does not measure the wealth of an economy by the number of transactions it records.

It measures it by its ability to create opportunities for the human beings who compose it.

Because before markets, before currencies, and before technologies, there are women and men who create, undertake, produce, exchange, and cooperate.

It is this conviction that guides HumChain Network.

Technology provides the infrastructure. Human beings create the economy.
