# Chapter 11: The Principle of Commercial Neutrality

### Introduction

HumChain Network is designed as a decentralized economic infrastructure, not as a crypto-asset trading platform.

Its mission is to organize economic exchanges between human beings, merchants, producers, artisans, businesses, institutions, and the various marketplaces built on the network.

The protocol does not seek to become a player in the financial exchange market. It provides the infrastructure upon which a sustainable digital economy can develop.

### The Separation Between Infrastructure and Market

HumChain Network deliberately distinguishes two complementary but independent functions.

The first is the creation of economic value through commerce, goods, services, and applications developed within the ecosystem.

The second is the exchange of HCN for other digital assets or local currencies.

These two functions address different needs and do not need to be provided by the same infrastructure.

### The Role of HCN

HCN is the native currency of HumChain Network.

It is intended to circulate within the ecosystem to enable payments, access to services, the operation of marketplaces, and all economic activities offered by the protocol.

Its primary purpose is to power a real economy built on the HumChain infrastructure.

### Asset Custody

Users keep their HCN in their own digital wallets.

HumChain Network does not custody its users' assets and does not act as a financial intermediary.

Each user remains fully responsible for holding and using their funds.

### Trading as an External Service

Trading, buying, selling, or converting HCN can be carried out on specialized platforms, whether centralized (CEX) or decentralized (DEX).

These platforms fulfill a function that is complementary to HumChain Network but are not part of its economic infrastructure.

The protocol remains neutral with regard to the platforms used by users.

### Benefits of the Commercial Neutrality Principle

This choice allows HumChain Network to focus its resources on its fundamental mission:

- developing an open economic infrastructure;
- fostering commerce and the exchange of goods and services;
- connecting economic actors to different marketplaces;
- strengthening local economies before their integration into a global economy;
- preserving the neutrality of the protocol with respect to trading activities.

Thus, HumChain Network is not designed to become an exchange. It is designed to become the economic infrastructure on which value exchanges find concrete utility in real economic life.

### The Economy Before Speculation

HumChain Network considers that value creation naturally precedes financial markets.

The value of a currency does not come solely from its price on exchange platforms, but above all from its utility in economic life.

That is why the protocol prioritizes the development of an ecosystem where HCN circulates to facilitate commerce, pay for services, access economic applications, and support exchanges between network participants.

The market can assign a price to HCN, but it is the economy that gives it lasting utility.

### An Open Infrastructure

The principle of commercial neutrality ensures that HumChain Network remains an open infrastructure.

The protocol does not impose any specific exchange platform and does not grant any privilege to a specific operator.

Every user remains free to choose the solutions best suited to their needs to acquire, hold, or exchange their HCN.

This neutrality promotes the interoperability of the network with the various actors of the digital ecosystem while preserving its independence.

### Focusing Resources on the Protocol's Mission

By choosing not to operate a trading platform, HumChain Network focuses its resources on the areas that constitute its core mission:

- developing a global economic infrastructure;
- improving tools for merchants, producers, artisans, businesses, and institutions;
- strengthening payment, visibility, and networking mechanisms;
- supporting the development of marketplaces built on the network;
- promoting the adoption of HCN as a currency of use in the real economy.

This orientation allows the protocol to evolve while remaining faithful to its fundamental objective: to connect human beings, territories, and economic activities within a single infrastructure.

### Commercial Neutrality and Technological Neutrality

The principle of commercial neutrality does not mean that HumChain Network isolates itself from the blockchain ecosystem.

On the contrary, the protocol is designed to be able to interact with various actors in the digital economy while remaining independent of their business models.

HumChain Network does not favor any exchange, trading platform, or financial intermediary.

Its role is to provide an open economic infrastructure on which users, merchants, businesses, and developers can create value.

### A Currency Meant to Circulate

HCN was not designed to remain idle in wallets.

Its function is to circulate within the infrastructure to facilitate economic exchanges.

Each use of HCN contributes to the functioning of the ecosystem: payment for goods and services, access to network features, opening and operating marketplaces, advertising, or any other economic activity developed on HumChain Network.

The protocol thus encourages the use of currency in the economy rather than its mere holding.

### Independence of the Protocol

By separating the economic infrastructure from trading activities, HumChain Network reduces its dependence on the speculative cycles of the markets.

The development of the protocol is based primarily on the growth of its economic activity, the adoption of its services, and the progressive integration of economic actors.

This approach makes it possible to build a network whose value is linked to its utility rather than to the evolution of its price on the markets.

### Long-Term Vision

The principle of commercial neutrality reflects a fundamental conviction of HumChain Network: a sustainable economic infrastructure must be built to serve human activity before serving financial markets.

The success of the protocol will therefore not be measured solely by the market capitalization of HCN or its trading volume, but also by economic indicators such as:

- the number of activated territories;
- the number of verified human beings;
- the number of merchants and businesses integrated;
- the number of marketplaces deployed;
- the volume of goods and services exchanged;
- the effective use of HCN in the real economy.

These indicators more faithfully reflect the mission of HumChain Network: to build a global economic infrastructure based on human activity, commerce, and territorial development.

### Conclusion

The principle of commercial neutrality constitutes a fundamental strategic choice for HumChain Network.

The protocol does not seek to become a trading actor. It builds an economic infrastructure where the native currency finds its value through its use in commerce, goods, services, and economic applications.

HumChain Network thus chooses to develop the economy before the market, convinced that a sustainable currency is based first on real economic activity before being based on its exchange value.
