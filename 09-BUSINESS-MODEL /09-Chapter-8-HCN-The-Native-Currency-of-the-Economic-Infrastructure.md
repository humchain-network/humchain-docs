# Part IV — Business Model
## Chapter 8 — HCN: The Native Currency of the Economic Infrastructure

### 8.1 A currency designed for the real economy

HCN is the native currency of HumChain Network.

Its purpose is not only to serve as a means of payment.

It constitutes the common economic unit that allows the different actors in the network to interact within the same infrastructure.

Each exchange, each service, and each economic activity developed on HumChain can rely on HCN as the native currency of the network.

This approach guarantees economic coherence between the different services of the ecosystem.

### 8.2 More than a currency, an economic mechanism

In HumChain, HCN fulfills several complementary functions.

It facilitates exchanges between participants.

It supports the operation of services developed on the infrastructure.

It participates in the economic mechanisms defined by the protocol.

It contributes to network security through the protocol mechanisms provided for this purpose.

Thus, HCN is not only used to transfer value.

It participates in the overall functioning of the economic infrastructure.

### 8.3 A currency that circulates with activity

HumChain is based on a fundamental principle:

"The sustainable value of a currency depends on its utility in an active economy."

HCN is designed to circulate within the ecosystem.

As new businesses, services, marketplaces, and applications join the network, opportunities to use it multiply.

This circulation promotes an economy where currency accompanies exchanges rather than remaining inactive.

The development of the ecosystem thus strengthens the economic utility of HCN.

### 8.4 Participation in the infrastructure

Holding HCN makes it possible to participate in the HumChain economy.

Using HCN means taking part in the exchanges that drive this economy.

Merchants can accept it as the native currency.

Users can use it to access services offered on the network.

Entrepreneurs can develop activities based on this common economic unit.

HCN thus becomes the link between the different actors in the ecosystem.

### 8.5 An economy based on circulation

HumChain favors an economy where value creation is based on exchanges.

Each transaction.

Each payment.

Each service.

Each economic activity.

Contributes to strengthening the dynamics of the ecosystem.

The objective is not to favor passive holding of the currency.

The objective is to encourage its use in service of the real economy.

### 8.6 A currency in service of a vision

HCN was not designed as an end in itself.

It is a tool in service of a broader ambition: to allow human communities, territories, and economic actors to participate in a more open, more inclusive global economy, more based on real activity.

In this vision, currency is not the starting point.

It is the instrument that accompanies exchanges created by human beings.

That is why HumChain states:

"Mining distributes the currency. Commerce gives it utility."
