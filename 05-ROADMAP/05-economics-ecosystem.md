# Phase 3 — Deployment of the Economic Ecosystem

### Objective

After the activation of the first territories and the launch of community mining, HumChain Network enters a new stage of its development: the commissioning of its economic ecosystem.

The objective is no longer just to distribute HCN, but to give it concrete utility in the daily lives of citizens, merchants, artisans, producers, businesses, and institutions.

This phase progressively transforms a currency distributed by the protocol into a currency used in a real economy.

### 1. Deployment of HumWallet

The official HumWallet is progressively made available to users.

It allows users to:

- receive HCN;
- send HCN;
- check their balance and transaction history;
- interact with ecosystem services.

HumWallet becomes the main entry point for users into the HumChain economy.

### 2. Launch of HumPay

HumPay is deployed to enable payments in HCN.

Users can pay for their purchases and merchants can accept payments directly within the ecosystem.

This infrastructure promotes the daily use of HCN and supports the development of economic exchanges.

### 3. Opening of HumMarket

HumMarket is progressively opened to economic actors.

The platform allows merchants, artisans, producers, entrepreneurs, and businesses to offer their goods and services to a local and then international customer base.

Each new store strengthens the utility of HCN and stimulates the circulation of the currency.

### 4. Deployment of HumAds

HumAds is launched to allow economic actors to promote their activities.

Advertising campaigns can be used to:

- develop the visibility of businesses;
- highlight products and services;
- announce events;
- support business growth.

HCN thus becomes a tool for commercial development.

### 5. Integration of Economic Actors

Particular attention is paid to the integration of real-economy actors.

The protocol progressively supports:

- merchants;
- artisans;
- producers;
- service providers;
- businesses.

Their participation is essential to make HCN a currency of use and not just a currency held.

### 6. Development of Local Exchanges

Territorial representatives encourage the use of HCN within communities.

The first local economic exchanges allow:

- strengthening of local economic circuits;
- support for local producers;
- creation of new commercial opportunities;
- preparation for inter-territorial exchanges.

This step fully brings to life the principle:

**"Mining distributes the currency. Commerce gives it utility."**

### 7. Strengthening of the Ecosystem

As activity progresses, new services can be developed to meet the needs of users and businesses.

The ecosystem evolves progressively without calling into question the fundamental principles of the protocol.

Each new service strengthens the utility of HCN and contributes to the growth of the HumChain economy.

### Expected Outcome

At the end of this phase, HumChain Network will have:

- an operational digital wallet;
- a functional payment infrastructure;
- an active marketplace;
- an integrated advertising service;
- a growing network of merchants and businesses;
- a local economy where HCN circulates daily.

This phase marks a major evolution: HCN ceases to be primarily a distributed currency to become a currency used in service of a real economy, built by human communities and progressively connected to a global economic infrastructure.
