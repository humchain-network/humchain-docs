# Phase 0 — Foundation and Preparation

## Objective

The foundation phase constitutes the base of HumChain Network.

Before any territory activation, any monetary creation or any technical deployment, the protocol must have solid foundations, shared and understood by its community.

HumChain Network considers that trust cannot be decreed.

It is built.

This first phase is therefore devoted to defining the vision, developing the fundamental rules and preparing the human ecosystem that will bring the protocol to life.

---

### Founding Documents

The first step is to establish the documents that will serve as a reference for the entire ecosystem.

These documents define the vision, principles, economic rules, governance and technical orientations of HumChain Network.

They include in particular:

- the White Paper;
- the Constitution;
- the Tokenomics;
- the Roadmap;
- the Yellow Paper.

Together, they form the reference framework of the protocol.

---

### Community Building

An economic infrastructure cannot exist without a community.

During this phase, HumChain Network progressively develops an international community sharing the same vision.

The objective is not to seek the greatest possible number of registrations.

The objective is to bring together people who understand the principles of the protocol and wish to contribute to its development.

The quality of the community takes precedence over its size.

---

### Territorial Organization

HumChain Network also prepares its territorial organization.

Representatives are progressively identified to support the development of communities in their respective territories.

This organization aims to create a close relationship between the protocol and local economic actors.

It prepares the necessary conditions for the future activation of territories.

---

### Preparation of Technical Infrastructure

In parallel with community building, technical work continues.

This phase includes in particular:

- protocol design;
- development of the blockchain architecture;
- implementation of proof-of-humanity mechanisms;
- development of the first applications of the ecosystem;
- audits and security tests.

Technology is developed to meet the needs of the community, not the other way around.

---

### Awareness and Education

The development of a global economy requires that future users understand the principles of HumChain Network.

Awareness, training and support actions are put in place to present:

- the mission of the protocol;
- proof of humanity;
- how HCN works;
- the responsibilities of participants;
- the opportunities offered to merchants, entrepreneurs and developers.

This approach promotes progressive and responsible adoption.

---

### Expected Outcome

At the end of this phase, HumChain Network has:

- a complete documentary framework;
- an engaged international community;
- a territorial organization in the process of being structured;
- a technical infrastructure ready for the next steps;
- a common vision shared by the first participants.

This phase does not mark the economic launch of the protocol.

It prepares the necessary conditions for its success.

It reflects the founding conviction of HumChain Network:

> **A sustainable global economy is not built by launching a technology, but by progressively building a human community capable of giving it meaning and utility.**
