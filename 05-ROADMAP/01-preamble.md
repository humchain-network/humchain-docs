# Roadmap - Preamble

## Preamble

A roadmap is not simply a calendar.

It translates how a vision becomes a reality.

For HumChain Network, building a global economic infrastructure does not consist only of developing a blockchain or launching a digital currency. It is above all about building a human community capable of creating value, cooperating and bringing this infrastructure to life.

That is why HumChain Network adopts a different approach from many technological projects.

The priority is not speed.

The priority is solidity.

Before launching a global economy, we need local communities.

Before activating territories, we need verified human beings.

Before distributing a currency, we need people capable of giving it utility.

Before developing applications, we need users, merchants, producers, artisans and entrepreneurs ready to use them.

This philosophy is summarized by a simple principle:

> **"We build the people before the tech."**

Technology constitutes the infrastructure.

Human beings build the economy.

The roadmap of HumChain Network therefore follows a logical progression.

The first step is to establish solid foundations: defining a vision, writing the founding texts, structuring communities and preparing territorial organization.

The second step is to progressively activate territories through proof of humanity, thus allowing the birth of the first local economies.

The third step is the deployment of technical infrastructure, so that users can make payments, develop businesses and access ecosystem services.

Finally, local economies are gradually connected to each other to form an open, transparent and decentralized global economic infrastructure.

This roadmap does not aim to freeze the future.

It defines a direction.

Some deadlines may evolve according to the needs of the protocol, technological progress or the realities of the territories.

On the other hand, the principles that guide it remain constant:

- proof of humanity as the foundation of monetary creation;
- priority given to local economies;
- human decentralization;
- transparency;
- cooperation between communities;
- building a global economy at the service of humanity.

Each step presented in this roadmap contributes to a single objective:

**Building a global economic infrastructure by strengthening local economies, one verified human at a time.**
