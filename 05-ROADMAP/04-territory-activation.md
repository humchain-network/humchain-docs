# Phase 2 — Territory Activation and Economy Launch

### Objective

The second phase marks the transition between preparation and operational functioning of HumChain Network.

After building the community and the territorial organization, the protocol enters its first economic phase.

The first territories are activated, the first communities officially join the network, and monetary creation begins in accordance with the rules defined by the Constitution and the Tokenomics.

This step constitutes the starting point of the HumChain economy.

### 1. Opening of Registrations

Registrations are open to users in accordance with the rules of Proof of Humanity.

Each participant follows the official verification process of the protocol:

- territory selection;
- provision of identity information;
- phone number verification;
- biometric verification;
- creation of the unique territorial identifier;
- definition of a personal PIN code;
- validation by the protocol.

Each verified user obtains the same status within HumChain Network and joins their territory under equal conditions.

### 2. Activation of the First Territories

When a first verified inhabitant joins a territory, it is officially activated.

This activation triggers the economic mechanisms provided by the protocol:

- creation of the maximum allocation of the territory;
- opening of the territorial treasury;
- launch of community mining;
- integration of the territory into the global economy of HumChain Network.

Activation does not confer any property rights over the territory.

It only marks the beginning of its economic development.

### 3. Start of Community Mining

After activation, verified inhabitants can participate in mining in accordance with the rules of the protocol.

Mining allows the progressive distribution of the HCN share reserved for the community.

It does not create new money.

It progressively puts into circulation the allocation planned for the territory.

This distribution accompanies the growth of the community and prepares future economic exchanges.

### 4. Structuring of the First Local Economies

Territorial representatives support the first users to foster the emergence of a true local economy.

Priorities include:

- integration of merchants;
- identification of producers and artisans;
- creation of the first local economic networks;
- preparation of the first payments in HCN.

The objective is for the currency to quickly find concrete utility in daily exchanges.

### 5. Preparation of Ecosystem Services

During this phase, the main services of HumChain Network continue their deployment to support economic activity.

This includes:

- the digital wallet;
- payment solutions;
- tools for merchants;
- user management interfaces;
- the first economic features of the protocol.

These services will allow activated territories to progressively develop their commercial activities.

### Expected Outcome

At the end of this phase, HumChain Network will have:

- the first territories officially activated;
- a community of verified users participating in mining;
- the first territorial allocations created according to the rules of the protocol;
- the first local economies under development;
- the necessary foundations for launching the economic services of the ecosystem.

This step embodies the founding principle of HumChain Network:

**Currency does not precede communities.
It is born with them, circulates among them, and derives its value from their economic activity.**
