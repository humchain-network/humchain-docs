# Phase 1 — Building the Human Network

### Objective

The first operational phase of HumChain Network is to build the human network that will serve as the foundation for the entire protocol.

Before the circulation of HCN and the launch of economic services, it is essential to create an organized community capable of understanding the project's vision and actively participating in its development.

**HumChain Network believes that true decentralization starts with people.**

### 1. Deployment of Territorial Representation

During this phase, the protocol progressively establishes its territorial organization.

Each territory is to be coordinated by a national representative, supported by representatives at the city and then commune levels.

This organization pursues several objectives:
- create proximity with local communities;
- support new users;
- spread the principles of HumChain Network;
- prepare for the activation of territories;
- strengthen cooperation between economic actors.

> A representative does not exercise power over a territory. He acts as a facilitator, an educator, and a coordinator serving his community.

### 2. Development of Local Communities

HumChain Network encourages the creation of active communities in each territory.

These communities bring together citizens, merchants, artisans, producers, entrepreneurs, developers, and anyone wishing to participate in the ecosystem.

The goal is to foster regular exchanges, share knowledge, and prepare for future economic activities.

**A strong local economy is built first on an engaged community.**

### 3. Awareness of Proof of Humanity

Proof of Humanity is the foundation of the protocol.

During this phase, awareness campaigns explain:
- why each person can only own one account;
- the role of biometric verification;
- the importance of the trust circle;
- the responsibilities linked to the status of a verified user.

This preparation builds trust before the official opening of registrations.

### 4. Training of Representatives

Representatives benefit from progressive training to master:
- the vision of HumChain Network;
- the principles of the Constitution;
- how HCN works;
- the mechanisms of Proof of Humanity;
- the territorial organization;
- best practices for supporting communities.

They thus become the first ambassadors of the protocol in their territories.

### 5. Preparation for Territory Activation

Each community is invited to prepare for the activation of its territory.

This preparation includes:
- identification of the first participants;
- organization of local communities;
- dissemination of official information;
- coordination with representatives.

The objective is for each territory to be ready to enter the HumChain Network economy as soon as activations open.

### 6. Development of the First Tools

In parallel with community development, the first tools for users are finalized.

These include:
- registration systems;
- identity verification mechanisms;
- account management tools;
- interfaces for representatives.

These tools are designed to ensure a simple, secure experience that complies with the principles of the protocol.

### Expected Outcome

At the end of this phase, HumChain Network will have:
- a network of representatives being deployed;
- structured territorial communities;
- users prepared for Proof of Humanity;
- tools enabling the opening of registrations;
- the necessary conditions for the activation of the first territories.

This step confirms the philosophy of HumChain Network:

**Communities precede territories.
Territories precede currency.
Currency precedes economy.
And economy is born from human beings.**
